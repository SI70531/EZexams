A good tax system is judged on two goals. Efficiency: a tax costs more than the money collected, because it creates a deadweight loss (people change what they do) and an administrative burden (time and money spent complying). The marginal tax rate drives incentives; a lump-sum tax has a marginal rate of 0 and is the most efficient. Equity: pay by the benefits you receive, or by your ability to pay (vertical and horizontal equity). The two goals often conflict.好的稅制看兩個目標。效率：稅的成本比收到的錢多，因為有無謂損失（人們改變行為）和行政負擔（報稅花的時間和錢）。影響誘因的是邊際稅率；定額稅的邊際稅率是 0，效率最高。公平：依受益多寡繳，或依負擔能力繳（垂直公平、水平公平）。兩個目標常常互相衝突。

★★★ Taxes and efficiency: the costs of a tax稅與效率：課稅的成本

Policymakers care about two goals: efficiency and equity. One tax system is more efficient than another if it raises the same revenue at a smaller cost to taxpayers. The costs of taxes to taxpayers:政策制定者在乎兩個目標：效率和公平。收到一樣多的稅、但納稅人成本較低的稅制比較有效率。納稅人負擔的成本有：

Cost成本What it is是什麼
1. The tax payment itself1. 稅款本身A transfer from the taxpayer to the government從納稅人移轉到政府的錢
2. Deadweight loss2. 無謂損失Taxes distort the decisions people make: the fall in taxpayers' well-being exceeds the revenue the government collects稅扭曲人們的決策：納稅人福利的減少超過政府收到的稅
3. Administrative burden3. 行政負擔What taxpayers bear as they comply with the tax laws納稅人為了遵守稅法所承擔的負擔
  • "People respond to incentives": taxes distort incentives, so people allocate resources according to tax incentives rather than true costs and benefits.「人們會對誘因做出反應」：稅扭曲誘因，人們依照稅的誘因、而不是真正的成本和效益來分配資源。
Example (p14): a tax on pizzaThe price of pizza is $5. Jack values a pizza at $8, Jane at $6. The government levies a $2 tax on pizza and the price rises to $7. Find the total surplus before and after the tax, and the deadweight loss.例子（p14）：披薩稅 披薩一個 $5。Jack 覺得值 $8，Jane 覺得值 $6。政府每個課 $2，價格漲到 $7。求課稅前後的總剩餘和無謂損失。
看解答

Before: Jack's surplus = 8 − 5 = $3, Jane's = 6 − 5 = $1, total surplus = $4.
After: Jack still buys, surplus = 8 − 7 = $1. Jane (value $6 < $7) stops buying. The government collects $2 on Jack's pizza. Total surplus = 1 + 2 = $3.
Deadweight loss = 4 − 3 = $1: Jane's lost surplus, which nobody gets.課稅前：Jack 剩餘 3、Jane 剩餘 1，總剩餘 4。課稅後：Jack 還買，剩餘 1；Jane 不買了；政府從 Jack 收 2。總剩餘 = 1 + 2 = 3。無謂損失 = 4 − 3 = 1，就是 Jane 消失的剩餘，沒有任何人拿到。作業第 3 題（擊劍課）是同樣的算法。

★★ Administrative burden行政負擔

  • Includes the time and money people spend to comply with tax laws (keeping records, filling in forms).包括人們為了遵守稅法花的時間和金錢（保存紀錄、填表）。
  • Encourages spending resources on legal tax avoidance, e.g. hiring accountants to exploit "loopholes" to reduce one's tax burden.鼓勵人們花資源做合法節稅，例如請會計師利用「漏洞」降低稅負。
  • It is a type of deadweight loss: resources are used up and nobody gets them.它是一種無謂損失：資源被用掉了，沒有任何人得到。
  • It could be reduced if the tax code were simplified, but that would require removing loopholes, which is politically difficult.把稅法簡化可以降低行政負擔，但要拿掉漏洞，政治上很難。

★★★ Should income or consumption be taxed?該對所得課稅，還是對消費課稅？

  • An income tax reduces the incentive to save: with a 25% income tax rate, an 8% interest rate becomes a 6% after-tax interest rate, and the lost income compounds over time.所得稅降低儲蓄誘因：所得稅率 25% 時，8% 的利率稅後只剩 6%，少掉的收入還會複利累積。
  • Some economists advocate taxing consumption instead: this would restore the incentive to save, which is better for people's retirement income security and long-run economic growth.有些經濟學家主張改課消費：可以恢復儲蓄誘因，對退休生活保障和長期經濟成長都比較好。
  • Consumption-tax-like provisions in the U.S. tax code: Individual Retirement Accounts (IRA) and 401(k) plans. People can put a limited amount of saving into them, and the funds are not taxed until withdrawn at retirement. So they make the U.S. system more like a consumption tax.美國稅法中像消費稅的設計：個人退休帳戶（IRA）和 401(k)。存進去的錢（有上限）退休提領前不課稅，所以讓美國稅制更像消費稅。

★★★ Taxes and equity: the benefits principle and the ability-to-pay principle稅與公平：受益原則與量能課稅原則

Equity means distributing the burden of taxes "fairly". Agreeing on what is fair is much harder than agreeing on what is efficient, but there are several principles people use.公平是把稅的負擔「公平地」分配。要對什麼是公平達成共識，比對效率難得多，但有幾個大家常用的原則。

Principle原則Idea想法Example例子
Benefits principle受益原則People should pay taxes based on the benefits they receive from government services. Tries to make public goods similar to private goods (用多少付多少).依照從政府服務得到的好處繳稅，讓公共財像私有財一樣：用多少付多少。Gasoline taxes: the tax paid is related to how much a person uses public roads.汽油稅：繳多少和用多少公共道路有關。
Ability-to-pay principle量能課稅原則Taxes should be levied according to how well a person can shoulder the burden; all taxpayers should make an "equal sacrifice".依照每個人承擔負擔的能力課稅，讓每個人做「同等的犧牲」。A $10,000 tax bill is a bigger sacrifice for a poor person than for a rich person.$10,000 的稅對窮人是比對富人更大的犧牲。

The ability-to-pay principle leads to two ideas of equity:量能課稅原則衍生出兩種公平：

  • Vertical equity: taxpayers with a greater ability to pay should pay larger amounts. Richer taxpayers should pay more than poorer taxpayers; the hard question is how much more.Vertical equity（垂直公平）：負擔能力越高的人應該繳越多。富人應該比窮人多繳，難的是要多繳多少。
  • Horizontal equity: taxpayers with similar abilities to pay should pay the same amount. The tax law has special provisions (deductions, 扣除額) that change a family's tax based on its circumstances. Problem: it is hard to agree on which factors besides income (e.g. medical bills, tuition) determine ability to pay.Horizontal equity（水平公平）：負擔能力相同的人應該繳一樣多。稅法用特別規定（扣除額）依家庭狀況調整稅額。難處：除了所得，哪些因素（例如醫藥費、學費）會影響負擔能力，很難有共識。

  • Suppose society is deciding the size of the government budget. Each voter has a preferred budget. Line up all voters in order of their preferences: the median voter is the one right in the middle.假設社會要決定政府預算的大小，每個選民有自己理想的預算。把所有選民照偏好排好，正中間那位就是中位選民。

★★★ Structural unemployment: the wage is kept above equilibrium (1. minimum-wage laws)結構性失業：工資被維持在均衡之上（1. 最低工資法）

Structural unemployment occurs when there are not enough jobs to go around, because the wage is kept above equilibrium: at the actual wage W₁ the quantity of labor supplied exceeds the quantity demanded. There are three reasons for this: minimum-wage laws, unions and efficiency wages.結構性失業發生在工作不夠分的時候，原因是工資被維持在均衡之上：在實際工資 W₁，勞動供給量大於勞動需求量。原因有三個：最低工資、工會、效率工資。

Why the wage stays above WE1. Minimum-wage laws: the law sets the floor.
2. Unions: collective bargaining pushes the wage up.
3. Efficiency wages: the firm itself chooses to pay more.
In all three the wage cannot fall to clear the market, so the surplus of labor stays.1. 最低工資法：法律訂下限。2. 工會：集體議價把工資推高。3. 效率工資：廠商自己選擇多付。三種情況工資都降不下來讓市場結清，勞動過剩就一直存在。
  • Minimum wage: may exceed the equilibrium wage for the least skilled or experienced workers, causing structural unemployment.Minimum wage（最低工資）：可能高於技能或經驗最少的工人的均衡工資，造成結構性失業。

1. Worker health工人健康In less developed countries poor nutrition is common; higher wages let workers eat better, so they are healthier and more productive.在開發中國家營養不良很常見；高工資讓工人吃得好、更健康、生產力更高。
2. Worker turnover工人流動Hiring and training new workers is costly; high wages give workers more incentive to stay, so turnover falls.雇用與訓練新人很花錢；高工資讓工人更想留下，降低離職率。
3. Worker quality工人品質Higher wages attract better job applicants, raising the quality of the workforce.高工資吸引較好的應徵者，提高員工素質。
4. Worker effort工人努力Workers can work hard or shirk; shirkers are fired if caught. Being fired is a good deterrent only if another job is hard to find; with the wage above equilibrium there aren't enough jobs to go around, so workers have more incentive to work, not shirk.工人可以努力也可以偷懶，被抓到就開除。開除有沒有嚇阻力，要看找新工作難不難；工資高於均衡時工作不夠分，工人就比較不敢偷懶。

(a) Effect on the demand for labor? (b) If employees value the benefit exactly at its cost, effect on the supply of labor? (c) If the wage adjusts freely, how does the law affect the wage and employment? Are employers and employees better or worse off? (d) If, before the mandate, the wage was $3 above the minimum wage, how does the mandate affect the wage, employment and unemployment? (e) If workers do not value the benefit at all, how do the answers to (b) and (c) change?Appendix Question 10（p67–71）：每小時 $4 的雇主強制福利 國會要求雇主提供某項福利（例如健保），讓每位員工每小時成本多 $4。(a) 對勞動需求？(b) 員工對福利的評價剛好等於成本時，對勞動供給？(c) 工資可自由調整時，工資與就業？雇主、員工變好或變差？(d) 原本工資比最低工資高 $3，對工資、就業、失業？(e) 員工完全不重視這項福利時，(b)(c) 怎麼改？
看解答

(a) Each worker now costs $4 more, so the demand curve shifts down by $4 (firms will pay a wage $4 lower for each quantity).
(b) Workers value the benefit at $4, so they will work the same hours for a wage $4 lower: the supply curve shifts down by $4.
(c) Both curves shift down by $4 → the wage falls by exactly $4 and employment is unchanged. Employers pay $4 less in wages plus $4 of benefit, the same cost; workers get $4 less in wages plus a benefit worth $4. Both are no better and no worse off.
(d) The wage can fall only $3 before it hits the minimum wage. The wage stays at the minimum wage, $1 above the new equilibrium → employment falls and unemployment rises (labor surplus).
(e) (b) The supply curve does not shift. (c) Only demand shifts down: the wage falls by less than $4, employment falls, and both employers and employees are worse off (a deadweight loss, like a tax).(a) 每位員工多花 $4，需求線往下移 $4。(b) 員工覺得福利值 $4，願意少拿 $4 工資做一樣多，供給線往下移 $4。(c) 兩條線都下移 $4 → 工資剛好降 $4、就業不變；雇主總成本一樣，員工工資少 $4 但多了值 $4 的福利，雙方不變好也不變差。(d) 工資只能降 $3 就碰到最低工資，停在比新均衡高 $1 的地方 → 就業下降、失業上升。(e) (b) 供給線不動；(c) 只有需求下移，工資降不到 $4、就業下降，雇主和員工都變差（像課稅一樣有無謂損失）。

Chapter summarySummary 本章總結
  • The unemployment rate is the percentage of those who would like to work who do not have jobs.失業率是想工作的人當中沒有工作的比例。
  • Unemployment and labor-force participation vary widely across demographic groups.失業率和勞參率在不同人口族群之間差異很大。
  • The natural rate of unemployment is the normal rate around which the actual rate fluctuates. Cyclical unemployment is the deviation from the natural rate and is connected to short-term economic fluctuations.自然失業率是實際失業率上下波動的正常水準。週期性失業是偏離自然失業率的部分，和短期經濟波動有關。
  • The natural rate includes frictional and structural unemployment. Frictional unemployment occurs when workers take time to search for the right jobs.自然失業率包含摩擦性和結構性失業。摩擦性失業是工人花時間找對的工作而產生。
  • Structural unemployment occurs when above-equilibrium wages result in a surplus of labor; three reasons: minimum-wage laws, unions and efficiency wages.結構性失業是工資高於均衡造成勞動過剩；三個原因：最低工資、工會、效率工資。
Common trap易錯點
  • The u-rate divides by the labor force; the participation rate divides by the adult population. Don't swap them.失業率除以勞動力，勞參率除以成年人口，不要搞反。
  • A discouraged worker is not in the labor force, so when someone gives up looking the u-rate falls (and the participation rate falls too).怯志工作者是非勞動力，所以有人放棄找工作時失業率下降（勞參率也下降）。
  • A part-time worker counts as employed, even if he wants a full-time job.兼職工作者算就業，就算他想要全職。
  • Sectoral shifts and unemployment insurance → frictional; minimum wage, unions, efficiency wages → structural.部門移轉、失業保險 → 摩擦性；最低工資、工會、效率工資 → 結構性。
  • Natural rate = frictional + structural (not cyclical). In a recession cyclical unemployment is positive; in a boom it is negative.自然失業率 = 摩擦性 + 結構性（不含週期性）。衰退時週期性失業為正，繁榮時為負。
  • Efficiency wages are paid voluntarily by firms; minimum wages and union wages are not the firm's choice.效率工資是廠商自願付的；最低工資和工會工資不是廠商自己選的。
  • Most spells of unemployment are short, but most unemployment observed at a moment is long term.大部分失業期很短，但某一時點看到的失業大多是長期的。
