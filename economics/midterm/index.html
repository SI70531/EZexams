How People Make Decisions · Principles 1–4

★★★ Principle 1: People face trade-offs (取捨): the philosophy of comparison

  • To get something we like, we usually have to give up another thing we also like.想得到一樣喜歡的東西，通常要放棄另一樣也喜歡的東西。
  • Personal examples: a party the night before an exam → less study time; wanting more money to buy things (slave to wants) → longer hours, less leisure; protecting the environment (limited resources) → resources cannot go to urban transit.個人例子：考試前一晚去 party → 讀書時間變少；想要更多錢買東西（慾望的奴隸）→ 工時變長、休閒變少；保護環境（資源有限）→ 資源不能拿去發展都市交通。
  • Social examples:社會例子：
    • guns and butter (大砲與奶油): the more spent on national defense (guns), the less is left for consumer goods that raise the living standard (butter).guns and butter（大砲與奶油）：國防（guns）花越多，能用在提升生活水準的消費品（butter）就越少。
    • Pollution control: a cleaner environment and better health, at the price of lower incomes for owners, workers and customers (protecting the environment raises product costs and lowers worker benefits).污染管制：環境更乾淨、健康更好，代價是廠商老闆、員工、顧客的所得減少（保護環境造成商品成本上揚、員工福利減少）。

The efficiency vs. equality trade-off (效率 vs 公平的取捨): to be fairer, the government redistributes income from rich to poor (taxes, welfare), but this reduces the incentive to work and produce, so the economic "pie" shrinks (high taxes lower willingness to work).效率 vs 公平的取捨：為了更公平，政府把所得從富人重分配給窮人（課稅、福利），但這會降低工作與生產的誘因，讓經濟這塊「餅」變小（高稅額會降低工作意願）。

★★★ Principle 2: The cost of something is what you give up to get it (機會成本)

  • Decisions compare the costs and benefits of each option, and cost is measured as opportunity cost (機會成本): whatever must be given up to obtain something (the value of the next-best alternative given up).做決策要比較各選項的成本與效益，而且成本要用 opportunity cost (機會成本)：為了得到某樣東西所必須放棄的東西（放棄的次佳選擇的價值）。
  • The opportunity cost of one year of college = tuition, books, fees + forgone wages (放棄的工資). Room and board do not count, because you would need housing and food anyway.上大學一年的機會成本 = 學費、書錢、雜費 ＋ 放棄的工資（foregone wages）。住宿和吃飯不算，因為不上學也要住要吃。
  • The opportunity cost of going to a movie = the ticket price + the value of the time you spend in the theater.看電影的機會成本 = 電影票價 ＋ 你待在電影院那段時間的價值。

  • explicit costs (外顯成本): money actually paid out (wages, materials, rent).explicit costs (外顯成本)：真的付出去的錢（薪水、原料、租金）。
  • implicit costs (隱性成本): nothing is paid, but something is given up (the salary the owner's own time could earn, the interest the owner's own funds could earn).implicit costs (隱性成本)：沒付錢但放棄了的東西（老闆自己的時間本來能賺的薪水、自有資金本來能賺的利息）。

★★★ Principle 3: Rational people think at the margin (邊際思考)

  • rational people (理性的人): systematically and purposefully do the best they can to achieve their objectives, given the available opportunities.rational people (理性的人)：在可用的機會下，有系統、有目的地盡力達成目標。
  • Decisions are made by weighing a marginal change (邊際變動): a small incremental adjustment to a plan. Rule: act only if marginal benefit MB (邊際利益) > marginal cost MC (邊際成本); the optimum is at MB = MC. Example: at a sushi restaurant, whether to take one more plate depends on the benefit and cost of that plate, not the average.做決策時評估 marginal change (邊際變動)：對計畫做「多一點或少一點」的小幅調整。決策法則：邊際利益 MB > 邊際成本 MC 才做；最適點在 MB = MC（邊際價值 = 邊際成本）。例：壽司郎（スシロー）要不要再拿一盤，看「這一盤」的好處和成本，不是平均。
  • Example 1: people with unlimited-minutes phone plans make long, boring calls because at the margin each minute is free (MC = 0), so they call whenever MB > 0. Similar: empty airline seats are sold cheaply because the marginal cost of carrying one more passenger is very low.例 1：手機吃到飽的人常打又長又無聊的電話——因為在邊際上每分鐘免費（MC = 0），只要 MB > 0 就會打（沒事也會打電話）。類似的例子：飛機空位用低價賣，因為多載一個人的邊際成本很低。
  • Example 2: a manager deciding whether to raise output compares the labor and material cost with the "extra revenue".例 2：經理考慮要不要增產 → 比較所需人工與原料的成本和「多賺的收入」。
  • The water–diamond paradox (水與鑽石的矛盾): water is a necessity yet cheaper than diamonds. Water is plentiful, so the marginal benefit of one more glass is small; diamonds are scarce, so one more has a large marginal benefit. Price reflects marginal value.水與鑽石的矛盾：水是必需品卻比鑽石便宜？因為水很多，多一杯水的邊際利益很小；鑽石稀少，多一顆的邊際利益很大。價格反映的是邊際價值。
In-class Question (p21): sunk cost (沉沒成本)A company has already invested $5 million in developing a new product that isn't finished. After a competing product launches, expected sales fall to $3 million. Finishing development and production costs another $1 million. Should it proceed? What is the most it would pay to finish development?課堂 Question（p21）：沉沒成本 公司已投入 $5 million 開發新產品但還沒完成。競爭品上市後，預期銷售額降到 $3 million。完成開發與生產還要 $1 million，該不該做？最多願意付多少完成開發？
看解答

The $5M already spent is a sunk cost (沉沒成本): it cannot be recovered whether or not you proceed, so it should not affect the decision. Look only at the margin: spending another $1M (MC) gets $3M (MB), 3 > 1 → do it. The most you would pay is $3 million (any completion cost under 3M is worthwhile).已經花掉的 $5M 是 sunk cost (沉沒成本)，不管做不做都拿不回來，不應影響決策。只看邊際：再花 $1M（MC）可以拿到 $3M（MB），3 > 1 → 應該做。最多願意付 $3 million（只要完成成本 < 3M 就划算）。

★★★ Principle 4: People respond to incentives (誘因)

  • incentive (誘因): something that induces a person to act (a reward or a punishment). Rational people compare costs and benefits, so behavior changes when costs or benefits change.incentive (誘因)：促使一個人採取行動的東西（獎勵或懲罰）。理性的人比較成本與效益，所以成本或效益一變，行為就會變。
  • Gas prices rise → consumers buy more hybrids and fewer gas-guzzling SUVs. Cigarette tax rises → less teen smoking.油價上漲 → 消費者多買油電混合車、少買耗油的 SUV。香菸稅提高 → 青少年吸菸減少。
  • The seat-belt problem: after seat belts were promoted, why did driver deaths not clearly fall? Because with a seat belt on, the cost of driving fast is lower → drivers drive faster and more carelessly → more accidents and more pedestrian deaths. Policy must consider indirect incentive effects.安全帶問題：推廣安全帶後，駕駛死亡率為什麼沒有明顯下降？因為繫了安全帶，開快車的代價變小 → 駕駛開得更快、更不小心 → 車禍次數增加、行人死亡增加。政策要考慮間接的誘因效果。

How People Interact · Principles 5–7

★★ Principle 5: Trade can make everyone better off (貿易)

  • Trade is not a contest with one winner and one loser. People can buy a greater variety of goods and services at lower cost.貿易不是一方贏、一方輸的比賽。人們可以用更低的成本買到更多樣的商品與服務。
  • Countries gain from trade and specialization (專業分工): they sell what they produce at better prices abroad (sell high) and buy from abroad what is cheaper than making it themselves (buy low).國家從貿易與專業分工得利：自己生產的東西可以在國外賣到更好的價格（賣高），也能從國外買到比自己做更便宜的東西（買低）。
  • Slide example "don't put all your eggs in one basket" (p29, United Daily News 2023/9/4): from March to July 2023 Taiwan imported 145.31 million eggs, 88.91 million of them (61%) from Brazil. The opposition criticized that Brazil had been declared a bird-flu area in May yet imports continued. → Trade has benefits, but over-reliance on a single source is risky.講義例子「雞蛋不要放進同一個籃子」（p29，聯合報 2023/9/4）：112 年 3–7 月進口雞蛋 14,531 萬顆，巴西就占 8,891 萬顆（61%）。藍營批評巴西 5 月已公告為禽流感疫區、中央還讓超思進口蛋。→ 貿易有好處，但過度依賴單一來源有風險。

★★★ Principle 6: Markets are usually a good way to organize economic activity (市場)

  • market (市場): a group of buyers and sellers (not necessarily in one place).market (市場)：一群買方與賣方（不一定在同一個地方）。
  • "Organizing economic activity" = deciding what to produce, how much, who produces, who consumes.「組織經濟活動」= 決定生產什麼、生產多少、誰生產、誰消費。
  • market economy (市場經濟): resources are allocated by the decentralized decisions of many firms and households interacting in markets (household decisions vs. firm decisions).market economy (市場經濟)：由許多廠商和家計單位在市場中互動、分散決策來分配資源（家計單位的決策 vs 公司行號的決策）。
  • Adam Smith, The Wealth of Nations (國富論, 1776): every household and firm is led by an invisible hand (看不見的手) to promote overall economic well-being, though that is not their intention; they simply take market prices as given (the price mechanism).Adam Smith《國富論》(The Wealth of Nations, 1776)：每個家計與廠商都像被一隻 invisible hand (看不見的手) 引導，增進整體經濟福祉。你的本意並非如此，但只能接受市場的價格（價格機制）。
  • Prices are set by the interaction of buyers and sellers and reflect both the value of the good to buyers and the cost of producing it. The invisible hand = prices guide self-interested households and firms to decisions that maximize society's economic well-being.價格：由買賣雙方互動決定，同時反映商品對買方的價值和生產成本。看不見的手 = 價格引導自利的家計與廠商，做出讓社會經濟福祉最大的決策。

How the Economy as a Whole Works · Principles 8–10

★★★ Principle 8: A country's standard of living depends on its ability to produce goods and services

  • Living standards differ hugely across countries and eras: rich countries' average income is over 10 times that of poor ones; the US today lives about 8 times better than 100 years ago.各國與不同時期的生活水準差距很大：富國平均所得是窮國的 10 倍以上；美國今天的生活水準大約是 100 年前的 8 倍。
  • The most important determinant is productivity (生產力): the quantity of goods and services produced per unit of labor input.決定生活水準最重要的因素是 productivity (生產力)：每一單位勞動投入所生產的商品與服務數量。
  • Productivity depends on the equipment, skills and technology available to workers. Other factors (unions, foreign competition) matter much less.生產力取決於工人可用的設備、技能、技術。其他因素（工會、國外競爭）對生活水準的影響小得多。
  • Policy implication: raise living standards through education, good tools and access to the best technology.政策意涵：提高生活水準要靠教育、好的工具、取得最好的技術。

★★★ Principle 9: Prices rise when the government prints too much money

  • inflation (通貨膨脹): an increase in the overall level of prices in the economy.inflation (通貨膨脹)：經濟中整體物價水準上升。
  • In the long run, inflation is almost always caused by excessive growth in the quantity of money, which lowers the value of money; the faster the government prints, the higher the inflation rate.長期來看，通膨幾乎都是貨幣數量成長過快造成的，貨幣價值因此下跌；政府印鈔越快，通膨率越高。

★★ Principle 10: In the short run, society faces a trade-off between inflation and unemployment

  • Over a year or two, many policies move inflation and unemployment in opposite directions. Other factors can make the trade-off better or worse, but it never disappears.在一兩年的期間，很多經濟政策會讓通膨與失業朝相反方向變動。其他因素可以讓這個取捨變得比較有利或不利，但取捨一直存在。
  • Process: print money → spending rises → prices rise, firms raise output and hire → inflation up, unemployment down.過程：印鈔票 → 消費增加 → 物價上漲、廠商增產並聘用員工 → 通膨上升、失業率下降。
  • In the long run, unemployment is unrelated to printing money (only prices rise).長期來看，失業率和印鈔票沒有關聯（只剩物價上漲）。

★★ Application: was your decision to go to college right? (Eye on the Benefit and Cost of School)

  • Cost (full-time student): tuition, books, other study costs + forgone earnings (放棄的所得).成本（全職學生）：學費、書錢、其他學習費用 ＋ 放棄的所得 (forgone earnings)。
  • The cost is paid now, while the benefit is spread over a whole working life. For most people the net benefit is large.成本發生在現在，效益分散在整個工作生涯。對大多數人來說淨效益很大。

★★★ PPF and opportunity cost: the slope (斜率)

  • Moving along the PPF = shifting resources from producing one good to another → society faces a trade-off: more of one means less of the other.沿著 PPF 移動 = 把資源從生產一種商品移到另一種 → 社會面臨取捨：多要一種就要犧牲另一種。

  • A bowed-outward PPF: increasing opportunity cost: the more of a good you make, the more of the other good you give up for each extra unit.往外凸 (bowed outward) PPF：機會成本遞增——某商品做得越多，每多做一單位要放棄的另一商品越多。

Why is it bowed outward? (p24–26 beer vs. mountain bikes)為什麼會往外凸？（p24–26 啤酒 vs 登山車）

  • Point A: most workers are brewing beer, even those who are better at making bikes. Moving a few people to mountain bikes costs little beer → the opportunity cost of mountain bikes is low. (A big brewery has many mediocre brewers, even bike specialists.)A 點：大部分工人在釀啤酒，連那些比較會做腳踏車的人也在釀酒。分幾個人去做登山車，啤酒產量掉不多 → 登山車機會成本低。（啤酒廠人很多，裡面總有釀酒技能普通、甚至專精做腳踏車的人。）
  • Point B: most workers are making bikes, and only the best brewers are left. Making more bikes means pulling away top brewers → beer falls sharply → the opportunity cost is high. (With few people left, each is elite, so output falls fast.)B 點：大部分工人在做腳踏車，留在啤酒廠的都是最好的釀酒師。再多做登山車就要調走頂尖釀酒師 → 啤酒大跌 → 機會成本高。（人少時個個都是菁英，分幾個人走產能掉得快。）
  • Conclusion: when workers have different skills, or resources (e.g., land suited to different uses) have different opportunity costs, the PPF bows outward.結論：當不同工人技能不同，或資源（例如不同土地適合不同用途）的機會成本不同時，PPF 往外凸。

Shifts of the PPF (p28): if technology improves for only one good, the PPF pivots outward along that axis; only if both improve (neutral) does the whole curve shift out in parallel.PPF 的移動（p28）：只有一種商品技術進步時，PPF 只往那個軸「轉」出去；兩種都進步（neutral）才整條平行往外。

  • The opportunity cost of one good is the reciprocal of the opportunity cost of the other.一個產品的機會成本是另一個產品機會成本的倒數。

At what price do both sides gain from trade?

In the textbook example, the US trades 700 tons of wheat for 110 computers → 1 computer ≈ 6.4 tons of wheat. The US gives up 10 tons to make one computer itself; Japan gives up only 5. The price must lie between the two countries' opportunity costs (5 to 10 tons of wheat) for both to gain.在課本例子中，美國用 700 噸小麥換 110 台電腦 → 1 台電腦 ≈ 6.4 噸小麥。美國自己做 1 台電腦要放棄 10 噸，日本做 1 台只放棄 5 噸。價格必須介於兩國的機會成本之間（5 到 10 噸小麥），兩國才都得利。

★★ Comparative Advantage and Trade（總結）

  • The gains from trade come from comparative advantage (differences in opportunity cost).貿易利得來自比較利益（機會成本的差異）。
  • When each country specializes in the good in which it has a comparative advantage: total output rises, the world's economic pie grows, and all countries can gain from trade.每個國家專業化生產有比較利益的商品時：所有國家的總產量變高、世界的經濟大餅變大、所有國家都能從貿易得利。
  • The principle of comparative advantage applies to countries and individuals, and economists use it to argue for free trade (自由貿易) between countries.比較利益原則同時適用於國家和個人，經濟學家用它來主張國家之間的free trade (自由貿易)。

SOP for computing comparative advantage算比較利益的 SOP
  1. Check whether the problem gives "hours per unit" or "units per hour".確認題目給的是「每單位要幾小時」還是「每小時做幾個」。
  2. Compute each person's opportunity cost of each good: given time → OC(X) = time for X ÷ time for Y; given output → OC(X) = output of Y ÷ output of X.算每人每種商品的機會成本：給時間 → OC(X) = X 的時間 ÷ Y 的時間；給產量 → OC(X) = Y 的產量 ÷ X 的產量。

  4. The trading price must fall between the two people's OCs.交易價格要落在兩人的 OC 之間。
Common trap (易錯點)易錯點
  • Absolute advantage looks at "fewer inputs"; comparative advantage looks at "lower opportunity cost". The gains from trade depend only on comparative advantage.絕對利益看「投入少」，比較利益看「機會成本低」。貿易利得只看比較利益。
  • When the problem gives output, do not compare outputs directly as opportunity costs; convert first.給產量的題目不要直接拿產量比機會成本，要先轉換。
  • A person can have an absolute advantage in both goods but cannot have a comparative advantage in both.一個人可能兩樣都有絕對利益，但不可能兩樣都有比較利益。
  • Trade makes the country as a whole better off, but some people at home (workers in industries competing with imports) become worse off.貿易讓國家整體變好，但國內某些人（被進口品競爭的產業工人）會變差。

★★★ Elasticity along a straight-line demand curve

  • A straight-line demand curve has a constant slope (each $1 increase cuts purchases by 2 units) but non-constant elasticity.直線需求線斜率固定（每漲 $1 少買 2 單位），但彈性不固定。

★★★ The Price Elasticity of Supply (供給價格彈性)

Price elasticity of supply = % change in quantity supplied ÷ % change in price (again with the midpoint method) Example: P rises 8%, Qs rises 16% → elasticity = 2供給價格彈性 = 供給量變動百分比 ÷ 價格變動百分比（一樣用中點法） 例：P 上漲 8%，Qs 增加 16% → 彈性 = 2

Roughly measures sellers' price sensitivity. The five types mirror demand: perfectly inelastic (=0, vertical), inelastic (<1, relatively steep), unit elastic (=1), elastic (>1, relatively flat), perfectly elastic (=∞, horizontal). Determinants of the elasticity of supply (供給彈性)

  • The easier it is for sellers to change output, the more elastic the supply: beachfront property is hard to expand → less elastic than the supply of new cars.賣方越容易改變產量，供給越有彈性：海濱房地產的供給很難改變 → 比新車的供給缺乏彈性。

Chapter summary (本章總結)Summary 本章總結
  • Elasticity measures how much Qd or Qs responds to a determinant. Price elasticity of demand = %ΔQd / %ΔP; < 1 inelastic, > 1 elastic.彈性衡量 Qd 或 Qs 對某個決定因素的反應程度。需求價格彈性 = %ΔQd / %ΔP；< 1 缺乏彈性、> 1 富有彈性。
  • Inelastic demand → a price rise increases TR; elastic demand → a price rise decreases TR.需求缺乏彈性 → 漲價 TR 上升；富有彈性 → 漲價 TR 下降。
  • Goods that are short-run, necessities, broadly defined, or have few close substitutes have more inelastic demand.短期、必需品、定義廣泛、相近替代品少的商品需求比較缺乏彈性。
  • Price elasticity of supply = %ΔQs / %ΔP, larger in the long run than in the short run.供給價格彈性 = %ΔQs / %ΔP，長期比短期大。
  • Income elasticity measures the response to income changes; cross-price elasticity measures the response to another good's price change.所得彈性衡量所得變化的反應；交叉彈性衡量另一商品價格變化的反應。

Common trap (易錯點)易錯點
  • Elasticity ≠ slope. A straight-line demand curve has a constant slope, but elasticity is high at the top and low at the bottom.彈性 ≠ 斜率。直線需求線斜率固定，但彈性上段大、下段小。
  • Unless the exam says otherwise, always use the midpoint method.考試沒說的話一律用中點法。
  • "Quantity falls but revenue rises" → inelastic. Revenue rises when price rises ↔ inelastic.「數量減少但收益增加」→ 缺乏彈性。漲價收益上升 ↔ 缺乏彈性。
  • Negative income elasticity = inferior good; negative cross elasticity = complements, positive = substitutes.所得彈性負 = 劣等財；交叉彈性負 = 互補品、正 = 替代品。
  • When supply is inelastic, an increase in demand mainly pushes up the "price".供給缺乏彈性時，需求增加主要推高「價格」。

Chapter summary (本章總結)Summary 本章總結
  • A price ceiling is a legal maximum price (e.g., rent control); it binds when below equilibrium and causes a shortage.價格上限是法定最高價（例：房租管制）；低於均衡時有約束力，造成短缺。
  • A price floor is a legal minimum price (e.g., the minimum wage); it binds when above equilibrium and causes a surplus; the labor surplus created by a minimum wage is unemployment.價格下限是法定最低價（例：最低工資）；高於均衡時有約束力，造成過剩；最低工資造成的勞動過剩就是失業。
  • Without market failure, the free-market allocation of resources is optimal, and policies that change it often make the economy worse.在沒有市場失靈時，自由市場的資源配置是最適的，改變它的政策往往讓經濟變差。
  • A tax drives a wedge between the price buyers pay and the price sellers receive, lowering the equilibrium quantity, whoever is taxed.稅在買方付的價格與賣方拿的價格之間打進楔子，讓均衡數量下降——不管課在誰身上。
  • Tax incidence does not depend on who is taxed; it depends on the price elasticities of supply and demand.租稅歸宿和課在誰身上無關，取決於供需的價格彈性。
Common trap (易錯點)易錯點
  • A ceiling binds only if it is "below" equilibrium, and a floor only if "above". The direction is easy to reverse.上限要「低於」均衡、下限要「高於」均衡才有約束力。方向容易背反。
  • When a ceiling binds, the quantity traded = the quantity supplied; when a floor binds, the quantity traded = the quantity demanded (take the smaller).上限有約束力時，成交量 = 供給量；下限有約束力時，成交量 = 需求量（取小的那個）。
  • Rent control's shortage is larger in the long run than in the short run (elasticity grows).房租管制長期短缺比短期大（彈性變大）。
  • A tax on buyers shifts D down; a tax on sellers shifts S up; the result is the same.對買方課稅 D 往下移；對賣方課稅 S 往上移；結果相同。
  • The less elastic side bears more. Perfectly inelastic demand → buyers bear everything.誰比較沒彈性、誰負擔比較多。需求完全無彈性 → 買方全部負擔。

  • welfare economics (福利經濟學): the study of how the allocation of resources affects economic well-being. It looks at the benefits buyers and sellers get from market trade and how society can maximize them.welfare economics (福利經濟學)：研究資源配置如何影響經濟福祉。看買賣雙方從市場交易得到的好處，以及社會如何讓這些好處最大。

★★★ Market Efficiency (市場效率)

Total surplus = CS + PS CS = buyers' value − what buyers pay (buyers' gain from participating in the market) PS = what sellers receive − sellers' cost (sellers' gain from participating) → Total surplus = buyers' value − sellers' cost (the money paid in between cancels out)Total surplus = CS + PS CS = 買方的價值 − 買方付的錢（買方參與市場的利得） PS = 賣方收到的錢 − 賣方的成本（賣方參與市場的利得） → Total surplus = 買方的價值 − 賣方的成本（中間付的錢互相抵銷）

Lecture: watch the bargaining scene from Pretty Woman to get a feel for CS and PS.講義：看電影《麻雀變鳳凰》(Pretty Woman) 的殺價片段體會 CS 和 PS。

  • Resource allocation in a market economy is decentralized, determined by the interaction of many self-interested buyers and sellers. Total surplus measures society's well-being and tells us whether the market allocation is efficient. If the market allocation is broadly satisfactory, the government's role should be limited to protecting property rights, national defense, etc.; if not, public policy may improve it.市場經濟的資源配置是分散的，由許多自利的買賣方互動決定。用總剩餘衡量社會福祉，判斷市場配置是否有效率。若市場配置大致令人滿意，政府角色應限於保護財產權、國防等；若不是，公共政策可能改善它。
  • efficiency (效率): an allocation of resources that maximizes total surplus: ① goods are consumed by the buyers who value them most ② produced by the producers with the lowest cost ③ raising or lowering output cannot increase total surplus.efficiency (效率)：資源配置讓總剩餘最大：① 商品由最重視它的買方消費 ② 由成本最低的生產者生產 ③ 增加或減少產量都不會讓總剩餘增加。
  • Besides efficiency, policymakers also care about equality (公平): whether economic benefits are distributed evenly among members of society.決策者除了效率也關心 equality (公平)：經濟利益是否平均分配給社會成員。

Evaluating the Market Equilibrium（D：P = 60 − 2Q、S：P = 15 + Q，均衡 P = $30、Q = 15）：

  • Who consumes? Buyers with WTP ≥ $30 buy and those below $30 don't → the free market gives the good to those most willing to buy it.誰消費？ WTP ≥ $30 的買方都買、< $30 的不買 → 自由市場把商品分給購買意願最高的人。
  • Who produces? Sellers with cost ≤ $30 produce and those above $30 don't → production goes to the lowest-cost producers.誰生產？ 成本 ≤ $30 的賣方都生產、> $30 的不生產 → 交給生產成本最低的人。
  • Is Q optimal? At Q = 20, the marginal unit costs $35 > the consumer's value of $20 → reducing Q raises total surplus (true for any Q > 15). At Q = 10, value $40 > cost $25 → raising Q raises total surplus (true for any Q < 15). Only the equilibrium Q = 15 maximizes total surplus.Q 是最適的嗎？ Q = 20 時，邊際單位的成本 $35 > 消費者的價值 $20 → 減少 Q 能讓總剩餘增加（任何 Q > 15 皆然）。Q = 10 時，價值 $40 > 成本 $25 → 增加 Q 能讓總剩餘增加（任何 Q < 15 皆然）。只有均衡 Q = 15 讓總剩餘最大。

Adam Smith and the Invisible Hand（《國富論》1776）

"It is not from the benevolence of the butcher, the brewer, or the baker that we expect our dinner, but from their regard to their own interest." "Every individual… intends only his own gain, and he is led by an invisible hand to promote an end which was no part of his intention… By pursuing his own interest he frequently promotes that of the society more effectually than when he really intends to promote it." → The invisible hand takes all buyers' and sellers' information into account and guides everyone in the market to the best outcome: economic efficiency. So free markets are usually the best way to organize economic activity.「我們能有晚餐，不是因為肉販、釀酒師或麵包師傅的善心，而是因為他們在乎自己的利益。」「每個人……只想到自己的利益，卻被一隻看不見的手引導，去促進一個他本來無意促進的目標。……追求自己的利益，往往比真心想促進社會利益時，更有效地促進了社會利益。」→ 看不見的手考慮了所有買賣雙方的資訊，把市場上的每個人引導到最好的結果：經濟效率。所以自由市場通常是組織經濟活動最好的方式。

★★★ Market Efficiency & Market Failure（市場失靈）

  • "The market equilibrium is efficient" rests on two assumptions: ① the market is perfectly competitive ② market outcomes affect only the buyers and sellers in this market. If the assumptions fail, the conclusion may fail.「市場均衡有效率」建立在兩個假設上：① 市場是完全競爭的 ② 市場結果只影響這個市場的買方和賣方。假設不成立時，結論就可能不成立。
  • market power (市場力量): a single (or a small group of) buyer or seller can control the market price → the market is not efficient.market power (市場力量)：單一（或一小群）買方或賣方能控制市場價格 → 市場沒有效率。
  • externalities (外部性): buyers' and sellers' decisions affect people who take no part in the market → from society's viewpoint, the equilibrium is inefficient.externalities (外部性)：買賣雙方的決策影響到完全沒參與市場的人 → 從整個社會來看，均衡沒有效率。

Common trap (易錯點)易錯點
  • CS is "between the demand curve and the price", PS is "between the price and the supply curve"; don't swap them.CS 是「需求線和價格之間」，PS 是「價格和供給線之間」，不要對調。

★★ Review of Chapter 6: the effects of a tax

★★★ What determines the size of DWL: elasticity

The greater the price elasticity of supply and demand, the larger the DWL of a tax.供給和需求的價格彈性越大，稅的 DWL 越大。

  • Inelastic supply: when the tax lowers PS, firms can hardly leave the market → Q falls only a little → small DWL. Chapter summary (本章總結)Summary 本章總結
  • A tax lowers the welfare of buyers and sellers, and the loss usually exceeds the revenue the government collects.稅讓買方和賣方的福利下降，損失通常超過政府收到的稅收。
  • The reduction in total surplus (CS + PS + tax revenue) is called the tax's deadweight loss, DWL.總剩餘（CS + PS + 稅收）的減少叫做稅的無謂損失 DWL。
  • A tax has a DWL because it makes consumers buy less and producers sell less, shrinking the market below the quantity that maximizes total surplus.稅有 DWL 是因為它讓消費者少買、生產者少賣，市場縮小到總剩餘最大的水準以下。
  • The price elasticities of supply and demand measure how buyers and sellers respond to price; the greater the elasticity, the larger the DWL.供需價格彈性衡量買賣雙方對價格的反應，彈性越大 DWL 越大。
  • As a tax increases, the DWL grows even faster.稅增加時，DWL 增加得更快。
  • As a tax increases, revenue first rises, and eventually falls because the market shrinks.稅增加時稅收先增加，最後因為市場縮小而減少。

CH1 Ten Principles of Economics（經濟學十大原理）

1-1

What economics studies: scarcity (稀少性)

  • scarcity (稀少性): society's resources are limited while wants are unlimited (limited resources, unlimited wants), so it cannot produce everything everyone wants.scarcity (稀少性)：社會的資源有限，資源有限、慾望無窮，不可能生產每個人想要的所有商品與服務。
  • economics (經濟學): the study of how society manages its scarce resources (later: allocating them through supply = demand equilibrium).economics (經濟學)：研究社會如何管理稀少資源的學問（後面會學到：用供給 = 需求的均衡來分配）。
  • Firms: how much to produce and how many workers to hire (productivity).廠商：生產多少、雇用多少工人（生產力）。
1-2

Principle 1: People face trade-offs (取捨): the philosophy of comparison

  • To get something we like, we usually have to give up another thing we also like.想得到一樣喜歡的東西，通常要放棄另一樣也喜歡的東西。
  • Personal examples: a party the night before an exam → less study time; wanting more money to buy things (slave to wants) → longer hours, less leisure; protecting the environment (limited resources) → resources cannot go to urban transit.個人例子：考試前一晚去 party → 讀書時間變少；想要更多錢買東西（慾望的奴隸）→ 工時變長、休閒變少；保護環境（資源有限）→ 資源不能拿去發展都市交通。
  • Pollution control: a cleaner environment and better health, at the price of lower incomes for owners, workers and customers (protecting the environment raises product costs and lowers worker benefits).污染管制：環境更乾淨、健康更好，代價是廠商老闆、員工、顧客的所得減少（保護環境造成商品成本上揚、員工福利減少）。
1-3

Principle 2: The cost of something is what you give up to get it (機會成本)

  • Decisions compare the costs and benefits of each option, and cost is measured as opportunity cost (機會成本): whatever must be given up to obtain something (the value of the next-best alternative given up).做決策要比較各選項的成本與效益，而且成本要用 opportunity cost (機會成本)：為了得到某樣東西所必須放棄的東西（放棄的次佳選擇的價值）。
  • The opportunity cost of one year of college = tuition, books, fees + forgone wages (放棄的工資). Room and board do not count, because you would need housing and food anyway.上大學一年的機會成本 = 學費、書錢、雜費 ＋ 放棄的工資（foregone wages）。住宿和吃飯不算，因為不上學也要住要吃。
  • The opportunity cost of going to a movie = the ticket price + the value of the time you spend in the theater.看電影的機會成本 = 電影票價 ＋ 你待在電影院那段時間的價值。
1-4

Principle 3: Rational people think at the margin (邊際思考)

  • rational people (理性的人): systematically and purposefully do the best they can to achieve their objectives, given the available opportunities.rational people (理性的人)：在可用的機會下，有系統、有目的地盡力達成目標。
  • Decisions are made by weighing a marginal change (邊際變動): a small incremental adjustment to a plan. Rule: act only if marginal benefit MB (邊際利益) > marginal cost MC (邊際成本); the optimum is at MB = MC. Example: at a sushi restaurant, whether to take one more plate depends on the benefit and cost of that plate, not the average.做決策時評估 marginal change (邊際變動)：對計畫做「多一點或少一點」的小幅調整。決策法則：邊際利益 MB > 邊際成本 MC 才做；最適點在 MB = MC（邊際價值 = 邊際成本）。例：壽司郎（スシロー）要不要再拿一盤，看「這一盤」的好處和成本，不是平均。
  • Example 1: people with unlimited-minutes phone plans make long, boring calls because at the margin each minute is free (MC = 0), so they call whenever MB > 0. Similar: empty airline seats are sold cheaply because the marginal cost of carrying one more passenger is very low.例 1：手機吃到飽的人常打又長又無聊的電話——因為在邊際上每分鐘免費（MC = 0），只要 MB > 0 就會打（沒事也會打電話）。類似的例子：飛機空位用低價賣，因為多載一個人的邊際成本很低。
1-5

Principle 4: People respond to incentives (誘因)

  • incentive (誘因): something that induces a person to act (a reward or a punishment). Rational people compare costs and benefits, so behavior changes when costs or benefits change.incentive (誘因)：促使一個人採取行動的東西（獎勵或懲罰）。理性的人比較成本與效益，所以成本或效益一變，行為就會變。
  • Gas prices rise → consumers buy more hybrids and fewer gas-guzzling SUVs. Cigarette tax rises → less teen smoking.油價上漲 → 消費者多買油電混合車、少買耗油的 SUV。香菸稅提高 → 青少年吸菸減少。
  • The seat-belt problem: after seat belts were promoted, why did driver deaths not clearly fall? Because with a seat belt on, the cost of driving fast is lower → drivers drive faster and more carelessly → more accidents and more pedestrian deaths. Policy must consider indirect incentive effects.安全帶問題：推廣安全帶後，駕駛死亡率為什麼沒有明顯下降？因為繫了安全帶，開快車的代價變小 → 駕駛開得更快、更不小心 → 車禍次數增加、行人死亡增加。政策要考慮間接的誘因效果。
1-6

Principle 5: Trade can make everyone better off (貿易)

  • Trade is not a contest with one winner and one loser. People can buy a greater variety of goods and services at lower cost.貿易不是一方贏、一方輸的比賽。人們可以用更低的成本買到更多樣的商品與服務。
  • Countries gain from trade and specialization (專業分工): they sell what they produce at better prices abroad (sell high) and buy from abroad what is cheaper than making it themselves (buy low).國家從貿易與專業分工得利：自己生產的東西可以在國外賣到更好的價格（賣高），也能從國外買到比自己做更便宜的東西（買低）。
  • Slide example "don't put all your eggs in one basket" (p29, United Daily News 2023/9/4): from March to July 2023 Taiwan imported 145.31 million eggs, 88.91 million of them (61%) from Brazil. The opposition criticized that Brazil had been declared a bird-flu area in May yet imports continued. → Trade has benefits, but over-reliance on a single source is risky.講義例子「雞蛋不要放進同一個籃子」（p29，聯合報 2023/9/4）：112 年 3–7 月進口雞蛋 14,531 萬顆，巴西就占 8,891 萬顆（61%）。藍營批評巴西 5 月已公告為禽流感疫區、中央還讓超思進口蛋。→ 貿易有好處，但過度依賴單一來源有風險。
1-7

Principle 6: Markets are usually a good way to organize economic activity (市場)

  • market (市場): a group of buyers and sellers (not necessarily in one place).market (市場)：一群買方與賣方（不一定在同一個地方）。
  • "Organizing economic activity" = deciding what to produce, how much, who produces, who consumes.「組織經濟活動」= 決定生產什麼、生產多少、誰生產、誰消費。
  • market economy (市場經濟): resources are allocated by the decentralized decisions of many firms and households interacting in markets (household decisions vs. firm decisions).market economy (市場經濟)：由許多廠商和家計單位在市場中互動、分散決策來分配資源（家計單位的決策 vs 公司行號的決策）。
1-8

Principle 7: Governments can sometimes improve market outcomes (政府)

  • Note it says "sometimes" improves: policies can also be badly designed or distorted by politics.注意是「有時」能改善：政策也可能設計不良，或被政治力扭曲。
1-9

Principle 8: A country's standard of living depends on its ability to produce goods and services

  • Living standards differ hugely across countries and eras: rich countries' average income is over 10 times that of poor ones; the US today lives about 8 times better than 100 years ago.各國與不同時期的生活水準差距很大：富國平均所得是窮國的 10 倍以上；美國今天的生活水準大約是 100 年前的 8 倍。
  • The most important determinant is productivity (生產力): the quantity of goods and services produced per unit of labor input.決定生活水準最重要的因素是 productivity (生產力)：每一單位勞動投入所生產的商品與服務數量。
  • Productivity depends on the equipment, skills and technology available to workers. Other factors (unions, foreign competition) matter much less.生產力取決於工人可用的設備、技能、技術。其他因素（工會、國外競爭）對生活水準的影響小得多。
1-10

Principle 9: Prices rise when the government prints too much money

  • inflation (通貨膨脹): an increase in the overall level of prices in the economy.inflation (通貨膨脹)：經濟中整體物價水準上升。
  • In the long run, inflation is almost always caused by excessive growth in the quantity of money, which lowers the value of money; the faster the government prints, the higher the inflation rate.長期來看，通膨幾乎都是貨幣數量成長過快造成的，貨幣價值因此下跌；政府印鈔越快，通膨率越高。
1-11

Principle 10: In the short run, society faces a trade-off between inflation and unemployment

  • Over a year or two, many policies move inflation and unemployment in opposite directions. Other factors can make the trade-off better or worse, but it never disappears.在一兩年的期間，很多經濟政策會讓通膨與失業朝相反方向變動。其他因素可以讓這個取捨變得比較有利或不利，但取捨一直存在。
  • Process: print money → spending rises → prices rise, firms raise output and hire → inflation up, unemployment down.過程：印鈔票 → 消費增加 → 物價上漲、廠商增產並聘用員工 → 通膨上升、失業率下降。
  • In the long run, unemployment is unrelated to printing money (only prices rise).長期來看，失業率和印鈔票沒有關聯（只剩物價上漲）。
1-12

Application: was your decision to go to college right? (Eye on the Benefit and Cost of School)

  • Benefit: enjoying student life now + higher income in the future.效益：現在享受學生生活 ＋ 未來比較高的所得。
  • Cost (full-time student): tuition, books, other study costs + forgone earnings (放棄的所得).成本（全職學生）：學費、書錢、其他學習費用 ＋ 放棄的所得 (forgone earnings)。
  • The cost is paid now, while the benefit is spread over a whole working life. For most people the net benefit is large.成本發生在現在，效益分散在整個工作生涯。對大多數人來說淨效益很大。

CH2 Thinking Like an Economist（像經濟學家一樣思考）

2-1

The Economist as a Scientist (經濟學家是科學家)

  • Economists play two roles: ① Scientists: try to explain the world ② Policy advisors: try to improve the world.經濟學家扮演兩個角色：① Scientists：試著解釋世界 ② Policy advisors：試著改善世界。
  • As scientists, economists use the scientific method (科學方法): dispassionately building and testing theories about how the world works (bold hypotheses, careful verification). Economists can rarely run experiments, so they often test with historical events (natural experiments).當科學家時使用 scientific method (科學方法)：冷靜客觀地 (dispassionate) 建立並檢驗關於世界如何運作的理論（大膽假設、小心求證）。經濟學家很難做實驗，常用歷史事件（自然實驗）來檢驗。
  • assumptions (假設): simplify a complex world so it is easier to understand (so the important facts stand out). Example: in studying international trade, assume only two countries and two goods.assumptions (假設)：把複雜的世界簡化，比較容易理解（看清重要的事實）。例：研究國際貿易時，假設只有兩個國家、兩種商品。
2-2

Model 1: circular-flow diagram (循環流向圖)

  • A visual model of the economy: it shows how money flows through markets between households and firms. To teach engineering students, MIT professor Paul Samuelson (薩繆森) used a picture of "water flowing through a system of pipes" to show how income circulates in the economy (slide p7).經濟體的視覺化模型：顯示錢如何在市場中透過家計單位和廠商流動。MIT 教授薩繆森 (Paul Samuelson) 為了教理工科學生，用「水在水管系統中流動」的圖說明所得如何在經濟體中不斷循環（講義 p7）。
  • Two types of decision makers: Firms (廠商) and Households (家計單位).兩種決策者：Firms (廠商) 與 Households (家計單位)。
  • Two markets：Market for goods and services (商品與服務市場)、Market for factors of production (生產要素市場，即投入)。兩種市場：Market for goods and services (商品與服務市場)、Market for factors of production (生產要素市場，即投入)。
2-3

Model 2: production possibilities frontier (生產可能線 PPF)

  • PPF: a graph of the combinations of output the economy can produce given its existing factors of production and technology.PPF：在現有的生產要素與技術下，經濟體能夠生產的產出組合的圖。
  • Lecture example: two goods (computers, wheat), one resource (labor, in hours), with 50,000 labor hours per month. One computer takes 100 hours; one ton of wheat takes 10 hours.講義例子：兩種商品（電腦、小麥）、一種資源（勞動，以小時計），每月有 50,000 勞動小時。做 1 台電腦要 100 小時、1 噸小麥要 10 小時。
2-4

PPF and opportunity cost: the slope (斜率)

  • Moving along the PPF = shifting resources from producing one good to another → society faces a trade-off: more of one means less of the other.沿著 PPF 移動 = 把資源從生產一種商品移到另一種 → 社會面臨取捨：多要一種就要犧牲另一種。
  • The slope of the PPF = the opportunity cost of one good in terms of the other. Slope = rise / run = −1,000 / 100 = −10 → the opportunity cost of 1 computer = 10 tons of wheat.PPF 的斜率 = 一種商品以另一種表示的機會成本。斜率 = rise / run = −1,000 / 100 = −10 → 1 台電腦的機會成本 = 10 噸小麥。
  • Rule of thumb: the (absolute) slope of the PPF is the opportunity cost of the good on the x-axis.口訣：PPF 的斜率（絕對值）就是「橫軸商品」的機會成本。
2-5

Shape and shifts of the PPF

  • Economic growth (經濟成長): more resources or better technology → more computers, more wheat, or any combination in between → the whole PPF shifts outward.Economic growth (經濟成長)：有更多資源或技術進步 → 能生產更多電腦、更多小麥或兩者之間任何組合 → PPF 整條往外移。
  • A straight-line PPF: constant opportunity cost (in the example, 1 computer is always = 10 tons of wheat).直線 PPF：機會成本固定（前面的例子：1 台電腦一直 = 10 噸小麥）。
  • A bowed-outward PPF: increasing opportunity cost: the more of a good you make, the more of the other good you give up for each extra unit.往外凸 (bowed outward) PPF：機會成本遞增——某商品做得越多，每多做一單位要放棄的另一商品越多。

Why Economists Disagree (經濟學家為什麼意見不同)

  • Economists have different scientific judgments about positive theories of how the world works (doubts about whether a theory is correct).對世界如何運作的實證理論有不同的科學判斷（對理論正確性感到懷疑）。

CH3 Interdependence and the Gains from Trade（相互依賴與貿易利得）

3-1

Interdependence (相互依賴)

  • Every day you depend on people around the world you have never met: hair gel from Cleveland, Ohio, a phone from Vietnam, a shirt from China, coffee from Kenya.每天你都依賴世界各地你沒見過的人：髮膠來自美國俄亥俄州 Cleveland、手機來自越南、襯衫來自中國、咖啡來自肯亞。
  • Principle 5 of Chapter 1, "trade can make everyone better off": this chapter explains why people (and countries) choose interdependence and how they gain from trade.第 1 章的原理 5「貿易能讓每個人都更好」——這章說明人們（和國家）為什麼選擇相互依賴，以及怎麼從貿易得利。
  • ASK THE EXPERTS: "Trade with China makes most Americans better off because they can buy cheaper goods made or assembled in China" → 100% agree. "Some Americans working in competitive industries (clothing, furniture) are worse off because of trade with China" → 96% agree, 4% uncertain. → Trade makes the whole better off, but not everyone.ASK THE EXPERTS：「和中國貿易讓大多數美國人變好，因為能買到在中國製造或組裝的更便宜商品」→ 100% 同意。「某些在競爭產業（衣服、家具）工作的美國人因為和中國貿易而變差」→ 96% 同意、4% 不確定。→ 貿易讓整體變好，但不是每個人都變好。
3-2

Textbook example: the US vs. Japan (two countries, two goods, one resource)

  • US: 25,000/100 = 250 computers, 25,000/10 = 2,500 tons of wheat.美國：25,000/100 = 250 台電腦、25,000/10 = 2,500 噸小麥。
  • Japan: 15,000/125 = 120 computers, 15,000/25 = 600 tons of wheat.日本：15,000/125 = 120 台電腦、15,000/25 = 600 噸小麥。
  • Which point on the PPF depends on the preferences of society. Without trade, consumption = production, so each country is stuck on its own PPF.要選 PPF 上哪一點，取決於社會偏好 (the preferences of society)。不貿易時，消費 = 生產，只能在自己的 PPF 上。
3-3

Production and consumption with trade (Active Learning 2, 3)

  • Production: the US produces 3,400 tons of wheat → 3,400 × 10 = 34,000 hours, leaving 16,000 hours → 16,000/100 = 160 computers. Japan produces 240 computers → 240 × 125 = 30,000 hours, all used → 0 tons of wheat (Japan fully specializes in computers).生產：美國生產 3,400 噸小麥 → 用 3,400 × 10 = 34,000 小時，剩 16,000 小時 → 做 16,000/100 = 160 台電腦。日本生產 240 台電腦 → 240 × 125 = 30,000 小時全部用完 → 0 噸小麥（日本完全專業化在電腦）。
  • Exchange: the US exports 700 tons of wheat to Japan and imports 110 computers from Japan. imports (進口): goods produced abroad and sold domestically; exports (出口): goods produced domestically and sold abroad.交換：美國出口 700 噸小麥給日本、從日本進口 110 台電腦。imports (進口)：國外生產、國內銷售的商品；exports (出口)：國內生產、國外銷售的商品。
  • Consumption = production + imports − exports:消費 = 生產 + 進口 − 出口：
3-4

Where do the gains come from? Absolute advantage (絕對利益)

  • absolute advantage (絕對利益): the ability to produce a good using fewer inputs. It measures the "input" cost.absolute advantage (絕對利益)：用比較少的投入生產一種商品的能力。衡量的是「投入」成本。
  • The US needs 10 hours per ton of wheat vs. Japan's 25 → the US has an absolute advantage in wheat; the US needs 100 hours per computer vs. Japan's 125 → the US has an absolute advantage in computers too.美國做 1 噸小麥 10 小時 vs 日本 25 小時 → 美國在小麥有絕對利益；美國做 1 台電腦 100 小時 vs 日本 125 小時 → 美國在電腦也有絕對利益。
  • The US has an absolute advantage in both! So why does Japan specialize in computers, and why do both gain? → Because each country can specialize in the good with the lowest opportunity cost.美國兩種都有絕對利益！那為什麼日本專門做電腦？為什麼兩國都得利？→ 因為兩國可以各自專門生產機會成本最低的商品。
3-5

Comparative advantage (比較利益) and opportunity cost

  • Another way to measure cost: opportunity cost. The opportunity cost of 1 computer = the amount of wheat that the labor needed for 1 computer could have produced.另一個衡量成本的方法：opportunity cost。1 台電腦的機會成本 = 做 1 台電腦所需的勞動本來能做的小麥量。
  • comparative advantage (比較利益): the ability to produce a good at a lower opportunity cost.comparative advantage (比較利益)：以比較低的機會成本生產一種商品的能力。
  • principle of comparative advantage: each good should be produced by the one with the smaller opportunity cost → Specialize according to comparative advantage。principle of comparative advantage：每種商品應由生產它機會成本較小的人來生產 → Specialize according to comparative advantage。

  • cost (成本): the value of everything a seller must give up to produce a good (including the opportunity cost of the seller's time). Cost is the seller's "willingness to sell": produce only if P > cost.cost (成本)：賣方為了生產商品必須放棄的所有東西的價值（包含賣方時間的機會成本）。成本就是賣方的「願意賣價」：只有 P > cost 才生產。
  • Example: three lawn-mowing sellers: Jack $10, Janet $20, Chrissy $35. Supply schedule: P $0–9 → 0; 10–19 → 1; 20–34 → 2; 35 and up → 3.例：除草業的 3 個賣方：Jack $10、Janet $20、Chrissy $35。供給表：P $0–9 → 0；10–19 → 1；20–34 → 2；35 以上 → 3。
  • At any Q, the height of the supply curve = the cost of the marginal seller (邊際賣者) (the seller who would leave if the price were slightly lower). It can also be called "marginal cost" (邊際成本).在任何 Q，供給線的高度 = marginal seller (邊際賣者) 的成本（價格再低一點就會離開的賣方）。也可以簡稱「marginal cost 邊際成本」。
7-5

Market Efficiency (市場效率)

  • Resource allocation in a market economy is decentralized, determined by the interaction of many self-interested buyers and sellers. Total surplus measures society's well-being and tells us whether the market allocation is efficient. If the market allocation is broadly satisfactory, the government's role should be limited to protecting property rights, national defense, etc.; if not, public policy may improve it.市場經濟的資源配置是分散的，由許多自利的買賣方互動決定。用總剩餘衡量社會福祉，判斷市場配置是否有效率。若市場配置大致令人滿意，政府角色應限於保護財產權、國防等；若不是，公共政策可能改善它。
  • efficiency (效率): an allocation of resources that maximizes total surplus: ① goods are consumed by the buyers who value them most ② produced by the producers with the lowest cost ③ raising or lowering output cannot increase total surplus.efficiency (效率)：資源配置讓總剩餘最大：① 商品由最重視它的買方消費 ② 由成本最低的生產者生產 ③ 增加或減少產量都不會讓總剩餘增加。
  • Besides efficiency, policymakers also care about equality (公平): whether economic benefits are distributed evenly among members of society.決策者除了效率也關心 equality (公平)：經濟利益是否平均分配給社會成員。
7-6

Market Efficiency & Market Failure（市場失靈）

  • "The market equilibrium is efficient" rests on two assumptions: ① the market is perfectly competitive ② market outcomes affect only the buyers and sellers in this market. If the assumptions fail, the conclusion may fail.「市場均衡有效率」建立在兩個假設上：① 市場是完全競爭的 ② 市場結果只影響這個市場的買方和賣方。假設不成立時，結論就可能不成立。
  • market power (市場力量): a single (or a small group of) buyer or seller can control the market price → the market is not efficient.market power (市場力量)：單一（或一小群）買方或賣方能控制市場價格 → 市場沒有效率。
  • externalities (外部性): buyers' and sellers' decisions affect people who take no part in the market → from society's viewpoint, the equilibrium is inefficient.externalities (外部性)：買賣雙方的決策影響到完全沒參與市場的人 → 從整個社會來看，均衡沒有效率。

CH8 The Costs of Taxation（課稅的代價）

8-1

Review of Chapter 6: the effects of a tax

  • A tax drives a wedge between the price buyers pay and the price sellers receive: the buyers' price rises, the sellers' price falls, and the quantity traded falls.稅在買方付的價格和賣方拿的價格之間打進一個楔子：買方付的價格上升、賣方拿的價格下降、交易量減少。
  • The effect is the same whether the buyer or the seller is taxed.不管課在買方還是賣方，效果都一樣。
  • No-tax equilibrium: P = PE, Q = QE. With a per-unit tax $T: buyers pay PB, sellers keep PS (PB − PS = T), quantity QT. Tax revenue = T × QT (the rectangle of wedge times quantity).無稅均衡：P = PE、Q = QE。每單位稅 $T：買方付 PB、賣方拿 PS（PB − PS = T）、數量 QT。稅收 = T × QT（楔子乘上數量的長方形）。
8-2

How a tax affects CS, PS and total surplus (the A–F areas method)

  • deadweight loss (DWL) (無謂損失) = C + E：由market distortionthe reduction in total surplus (caused by a market distortion such as a tax).deadweight loss (DWL) (無謂損失) = C + E：由market distortion（市場扭曲，例如稅）造成的總剩餘減少。
  • B + D is merely transferred from buyers and sellers to the government, so total surplus doesn't shrink on that part; C + E is a loss that nobody receives.B + D 只是從買賣雙方移轉給政府，社會總剩餘沒少；C + E 是誰都沒拿到的損失。
  • The source of DWL: the units QE − QT are not sold because of the tax; for these trades the buyer's value exceeds the seller's cost, so they would have benefited both sides. The tax shrinks the market below the quantity that maximizes total surplus.DWL 的來源：QE − QT 這些單位因為稅而沒有被賣出——這些交易買方的價值高於賣方的成本，本來對雙方都有利。稅讓市場縮小到總剩餘最大的數量以下。
8-3

What determines the size of DWL: elasticity

  • Inelastic supply: when the tax lowers PS, firms can hardly leave the market → Q falls only a little → small DWL. The more elastic the supply, the easier it is for firms to leave → Q falls more → the larger the DWL (the same tax causes a larger DWL).供給缺乏彈性：稅讓 PS 下降時，廠商很難離開市場 → Q 只減少一點 → DWL 小。供給越有彈性，廠商越容易離開 → Q 掉越多 → DWL 越大（相同稅率造成更大的 DWL）。
  • Inelastic demand: when the tax raises PB, consumers can hardly leave the market → small DWL. The more elastic the demand → the easier it is for buyers to leave (fewer people are willing to buy) → the larger the DWL.需求缺乏彈性：稅讓 PB 上升時，消費者很難離開市場 → DWL 小。需求越有彈性 → 買方越容易離開（願意買的人變少了）→ DWL 越大。
8-4

How big should government be? The DWL of the labor income tax

  • Bigger government → more services, but higher taxes are needed → larger DWL. The larger the DWL, the stronger the case for small government.政府越大 → 提供越多服務，但需要更高的稅 → DWL 越大。DWL 越大，小政府的理由越強。
  • The labor income tax (勞動所得稅) matters especially: it is the government's biggest source of revenue; the US marginal tax rate (邊際稅率) is about 40%.labor income tax (勞動所得稅) 特別重要：是政府最大的收入來源；美國的 marginal tax rate (邊際稅率) 約 40%。
  • View 1: labor supply is quite inelastic (almost vertical; most people work full time whatever the wage) → the DWL of a labor tax is small.看法一：勞動供給相當缺乏彈性（幾乎垂直，不管工資多少大部分人都做全職）→ 勞動稅的 DWL 小。
8-5

As the tax grows: DWL and tax revenue

  • When the tax rises, DWL rises faster than the tax: double the tax and the DWL more than doubles (4 times with linear supply and demand); triple the tax and DWL more than triples (9 times). This is because the base and height of the DWL triangle both grow with the tax (DWL ∝ T²).稅增加 → DWL 增加得比稅還快：稅加倍，DWL 超過兩倍（線性供需下是 4 倍）；稅變 3 倍，DWL 超過三倍（9 倍）。因為 DWL 三角形的底和高都隨稅變大（DWL ∝ T²）。
  • Implication: when tax rates are low, raising them does little harm and cutting them does little good; when tax rates are high, raising them again is very harmful and cutting them is very beneficial.意涵：稅率低時，提高稅率傷害不大、降稅好處也不大；稅率高時，再提高非常有害、降稅非常有益。
  • Tax revenue: when the tax is small, raising it increases revenue; when the tax is large, raising it shrinks the market so much that revenue falls (the rectangle at 3T can be smaller than the one at 2T).稅收：稅小時，提高稅讓稅收增加；稅大時，提高稅讓市場大幅縮小，稅收反而減少（3T 的長方形可能比 2T 的還小）。
8-6

Eye on Congress: can Congress decide who pays a tax?

  • Congress requires employers and workers to pay the same Social Security tax (7.65% each in 2015), but because labor demand is much more elastic than labor supply, most of the tax ends up borne by workers.國會規定雇主和勞工繳一樣的社會安全稅（2015 年各 7.65%），但因為勞動需求彈性遠大於勞動供給彈性，最後大部分稅由勞工負擔。
  • A 20% income tax shifts labor supply from LS to LS + tax: the pre-tax wage rises $1 to $20/hour, the after-tax wage falls $3 to $16/hour, and weekly hours fall from 40 to 35 → workers bear 75%, employers 25%.20% 所得稅讓勞動供給從 LS 移到 LS + tax：稅前工資漲 $1 到 $20/小時，稅後工資跌 $3 到 $16/小時，每週工時從 40 降到 35 → 勞工負擔 75%、雇主 25%。
  • One thing Congress can do: pass tax laws that don't affect "marginal" decisions. The "Making Work Pay" tax credit (稅額抵減) in the 2009 American Recovery and Reinvestment Act ($400 for singles, $800 for couples) is a fixed credit that doesn't affect the choice of hours.國會能做的一件事：通過不影響「邊際」決策的稅法。2009 年《美國復甦與再投資法》的 "Making Work Pay" tax credit（單身 $400、夫妻 $800）是固定金額的稅額抵減，不影響工時選擇。

CH9 International Trade（國際貿易）

9-1

Review and the determinants of trade

  • Review of Chapter 3: a country that produces a good at a lower opportunity cost has a comparative advantage in it; countries export the goods in which they have a comparative advantage and gain from trade.複習第 3 章：一國若以較低機會成本生產某商品，就在該商品有比較利益；各國出口有比較利益的商品，就能從貿易得利。
  • Equilibrium without trade: only domestic buyers and sellers; price and quantity are determined by the domestic market, and total benefit = CS + PS.沒有貿易時的均衡：只有國內買賣雙方，價格與數量由國內市場決定，總利益 = CS + PS。
  • world price (世界價格) PW: the price in the world market. domestic price PD: the domestic price without trade.world price (世界價格) PW：世界市場上的價格。domestic price PD：沒有貿易時的國內價格。
9-2

Gainers and losers in an exporting country (the soybean example)

  • No trade: PD = $4, Q = 500. World price PW = $6 → the domestic price becomes $6: domestic consumers demand 300, domestic producers supply 750 → exports 450.沒有貿易：PD = $4、Q = 500。世界價格 PW = $6 → 國內價格變成 $6：國內消費者需求 300、國內生產者供給 750 → 出口 450。
  • No trade: CS = A + B, PS = C, total surplus = A + B + C.沒有貿易：CS = A + B、PS = C、總剩餘 = A + B + C。
  • 有貿易：CS = A（減少 B）、PS = B + C + D(adds B + D), total surplus = A + B + C + D → gains from trade = D。有貿易：CS = A（減少 B）、PS = B + C + D（增加 B + D）、總剩餘 = A + B + C + D → gains from trade = D。

Gainers and losers in an importing country (Active Learning 1: plasma TVs)

  • No trade: PD = $3,000, Q = 400; PW = $1,500 → domestic demand 600, domestic supply 200 → imports 400.沒有貿易：PD = $3,000、Q = 400；PW = $1,500 → 國內需求 600、國內供給 200 → 進口 400。
  • No trade: CS = A, PS = B + C, total surplus = A + B + C.沒有貿易：CS = A、PS = B + C、總剩餘 = A + B + C。
  • With trade: CS = A + B + D, PS = C, total surplus = A + B + C + D → gains from trade = D. B is transferred from producers to consumers.有貿易：CS = A + B + D、PS = C、總剩餘 = A + B + C + D → 貿易利得 = D。B 從生產者移轉給消費者。
9-4

Other benefits of trade, and why some people oppose it

  • Other benefits: consumers get greater variety; producers lower costs through economies of scale (規模經濟); more competition reduces domestic firms' market power (total welfare rises); the exchange of ideas speeds the spread of technology worldwide.其他好處：消費者有更多樣的商品；生產者因economies of scale (規模經濟) 降低成本；競爭增加，減少國內廠商的市場力量（總福利增加）；想法交流，加速技術進步在全世界散播。
  • ASK THE EXPERTS, "Past major trade agreements have benefited most Americans" → 93% agree, 0% disagree, 7% uncertain.ASK THE EXPERTS「過去主要的貿易協定讓大多數美國人受益」→ 93% 同意、0% 不同意、7% 不確定。
  • So why do so many oppose trade? Losers have more incentive to organize and lobby to restrict trade: the losses are concentrated on a small group and strongly felt; the gains are spread over many people who may not see the benefit of trade (10 million people each gaining $10 vs. 100 people each losing $100,000). In theory the winners could compensate the losers and still be better off, but such compensation rarely happens.那為什麼這麼多人反對貿易？輸家比較有誘因組織起來遊說限制貿易：損失集中在一小群人身上、感受強烈；利得分散在很多人身上、可能看不出貿易對他們的好處（1,000 萬人每人收益增加 10 元 vs. 100 人每人收益減少 10 萬元）。贏家理論上可以補償輸家後仍然變好，但這種補償很少發生。
