Help

Check the questions below first.

Sign-in and sync

Do I have to sign in?

No. The sign-in screen has “Skip sync, use this device only”: your answers are saved in this browser. Once you sign in, answers, scores and mistakes sync between your phone and computer.

What if I forget my password?

It can't be recovered. There is no email on file, so a password cannot be reset. Keep your username and password somewhere safe.

How is the PIN different from the password?

The password works on any device. The PIN is 4 digits and works only on the device where you set it, for quicker sign-in. After 5 wrong tries it is removed, and you must sign in with the password again.

Grading

What is the difference between keyword matching and AI grading?

Keyword matching only checks whether the answer contains key terms; it doesn't understand the sentences. AI grading reads your answer and gives a score with a Chinese comment. Use AI grading for written questions.

How do I turn on AI grading?

Usually it works as soon as you sign in. If it doesn't, go to the Grading section on the home page, paste a free key, and follow the three steps on screen. No credit card needed.

What if I see “Free quota used up for now”?

The free quota has a per-minute limit. Wait a minute or two and try again. Your answers are kept and unaffected.

Practice and mistakes

How do I see only the questions I have not answered yet?

Above each set of multiple-choice questions is “Practice mode”: All or Unanswered. Switching modes does not delete any answers.

Where do my mistakes go?

Wrong answers are placed after the matching key-point section in the notes, and that heading turns red. If no matching section is found, the question goes under “Other mistakes” for its chapter, so reread the whole chapter.

Can I undo clearing an answer?

Yes. Pressing “Clear answer” or “Redo chapter” starts a 3-second countdown. Press “Cancel” before it ends to keep your answer. Leaving the page also cancels the clear.

Display and motion

Too much animation? Can I turn it off?

In the theme button menu at the top right, choose “Reduced motion” under “Motion”. The setting is saved in this browser. The intro animation plays each time you open the sign-in screen; click it to skip. “Reduced motion” turns it off completely.

Does it work offline?

Pages you have opened are cached, so you can keep reading and answering offline. An internet connection is needed only for syncing and AI grading.
