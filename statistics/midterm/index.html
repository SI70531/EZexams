1.1 什麼是統計

  • Statistics as data (numerical facts): mean, median, percentages, maximum, etc.統計數據（numerical facts）：平均數、中位數、百分比、最大值等。

Decision rule:判斷口訣：

  1. Is it a label? → Yes → Is there an order? Yes = ordinal, no = nominal.是標籤嗎？→ 是 → 有順序嗎？有＝順序，沒有＝名目。

1.5 橫斷面資料 vs 時間序列資料

  • Cross-sectional: many entities at the same point in time. Example: building permits issued by each Taiwan county/city in September 2025.橫斷面（cross-sectional）：同一時間點、多個對象。例：2025 年 9 月台灣各縣市核發的建照數。
  • Time series: one entity over many points in time. Example: monthly building permits in Taipei over the past 36 months. Used to see trends and forecast.時間序列（time series）：同一對象、多個時間點。例：台北市過去 36 個月每月建照數。用來看趨勢、預測。

1.6 資料來源

  • Existing data: internal company records, commercial databases, government agencies, trade associations, special-interest groups, the internet.既有資料：公司內部紀錄、商業資料庫、政府機關、產業公會、特殊團體、網路。
  • Statistical studies統計研究
    • Observational study: variables are not controlled, e.g., surveys, smokers vs non-smokers studies.觀察研究（observational）：不控制變數，例如問卷、吸菸者 vs 非吸菸者研究。
    • Experimental study: first identify the target variable, then control the other variables, e.g., randomized controlled trial (RCT).實驗研究（experimental）：先確定目標變數，再控制其他變數，例如隨機對照試驗（RCT）。
  • Considerations in obtaining data: time, cost, data errors.取得資料的考量：時間、成本、資料錯誤。

1.7 敘述統計 vs 推論統計

  • Descriptive statistics: summarize data with tables, graphs, and numerical measures (e.g., Hudson Auto: mean parts cost of 50 invoices is $79).敘述統計：用表格、圖形、數值摘要資料（例：Hudson Auto 50 張發票平均零件費 $79）。
  • Population: all the entities of interest; sample: a subset of the population.母體（population）：研究對象的全體；樣本（sample）：母體的子集合。
  • Census: survey the entire population; sample survey: survey only a sample.普查（census）：調查整個母體；抽樣調查（sample survey）：只調查樣本。
  • Statistical inference: use sample data to estimate population characteristics or test hypotheses.統計推論：用樣本資料去估計母體特徵或檢定假設。

1.8 Analytics（分析學）

  • Descriptive analytics: what happened in the past.描述性分析：過去發生了什麼。
  • Predictive analytics: build models from past data to predict the future.預測性分析：用過去資料建模預測未來。
  • Prescriptive analytics: recommend the best course of action.處方性分析：給出最佳行動方案。

Ch2 表格與圖形

Frequency distributions, bar charts and histograms, skewness, cumulative distributions, stem-and-leaf displays, crosstabulations and scatter diagrams.次數分配、長條圖與直方圖、偏態、累積分配、莖葉圖、交叉表與散佈圖。

2.1 類別資料的整理

  • Frequency distribution: how many items fall in each category.次數分配（frequency distribution）：每一類別有幾個。
  • Relative frequency = frequency ÷ total; percent frequency = relative frequency × 100.相對次數 ＝ 次數 ÷ 總數；百分比次數 ＝ 相對次數 × 100。
    • Why relative frequency? Hotel A has 10 Excellent out of 20 reviews (50%), Hotel B has 50 out of 200 (25%). Counts alone mislead.為什麼要相對次數？Hotel A 20 則評價中 10 則 Excellent（50%），Hotel B 200 則中 50 則（25%）。只看次數會誤判。
  • Bar chart: for categorical data, bars are separated.長條圖（bar chart）：類別資料，長條之間分開。
  • Pie chart: relative frequency × 360° = sector angle (0.25 → 90°). Suitable when there are few categories, clear differences, and you want to show parts of a whole.圓餅圖（pie chart）：相對次數 × 360° ＝ 扇形角度（0.25 → 90°）。適合類別少、差異明顯、呈現「部分占整體」。

2.2 數量資料的整理

Three steps to build a frequency distribution:建立次數分配的三步驟：

  1. Decide the number of classes: usually 5 to 20.決定組數：通常 5～20 組。
  2. Decide the class width: width ≈ (largest value − smallest value) ÷ number of classes, rounded to a convenient number. Hudson: (109 − 52) ÷ 6 = 9.5 → use 10.決定組距：組距 ≈（最大值 − 最小值）÷ 組數，取方便的整數。Hudson：（109 − 52）÷ 6 = 9.5 → 取 10。
  3. Decide the class limits: classes must be non-overlapping and cover all the data.決定組限：各組不重疊，且涵蓋所有資料。

2.3 兩個變數：交叉表（Crosstabulation）

  • Two variables can be: categorical + categorical, categorical + quantitative, quantitative + quantitative (quantitative variables must be grouped first).兩變數可以是：類別＋類別、類別＋數量、數量＋數量（數量變數要先分組）。
  • Simpson's paradox: a conclusion from grouped data is completely reversed after aggregation. The cause is a lurking variable (e.g., very different sample sizes across groups). Look at the grouped tables before the aggregated table.辛普森悖論（Simpson’s paradox）：分組資料的結論在合併（aggregated）後完全反轉。原因是存在隱藏變數（例如各組樣本數差很多）。看合併表前要先看分組表。

2.4 兩個變數：圖形

  • Scatter diagram + trendline: the relationship between two quantitative variables. Positive (study hours vs grades), negative (price vs quantity demanded), no clear relationship (shoe size vs grades).散佈圖（scatter diagram）＋趨勢線：兩個數量變數的關係。正相關（讀書時數 vs 成績）、負相關（價格 vs 需求量）、無明顯關係（鞋碼 vs 成績）。
  • Side-by-side bar chart: compare differences between groups.群組長條圖（side-by-side）：比較各組之間的差異。
  • Stacked bar chart: see totals and composition; with percentages, all bars are equal in height at 100%.堆疊長條圖（stacked）：看總量與組成；若用百分比，所有長條等高到 100%。

4.1 隨機實驗、樣本空間、計數法則

  • Probability: between 0 and 1; the closer to 1, the more likely to occur.機率：0 到 1 之間，越接近 1 越可能發生。
  • Random experiment: a process that generates well-defined outcomes; repeating it may give different results.隨機實驗：產生明確結果的過程，重複做結果可能不同。
  • Sample space S: the set of all experimental outcomes; each outcome is a sample point.樣本空間 S：所有實驗結果的集合；每個結果叫樣本點。
  • Counting rule for multiple-step experiments: (n₁)(n₂)…(nₖ). Bradley: Markley Oil 4 outcomes × Collins Mining 2 outcomes = 8 outcomes. Represent with a tree diagram.多步驟實驗計數法則：(n₁)(n₂)…(nₖ)。Bradley：Markley Oil 4 種 × Collins Mining 2 種 = 8 種結果。用樹狀圖表示。
  • Combinations (order does not matter): C(N, n) = N! / [n!(N − n)!]. Example: choose 2 of 5 people for a team; Amy+Ben = Ben+Amy.組合（順序不重要）：C(N, n) = N! / [n!(N − n)!]。例：從 5 人選 2 人組小組，Amy＋Ben ＝ Ben＋Amy。

  • Conditional probability: P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B), "the probability of A given that B has occurred."條件機率：P(A｜B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)，「已知 B 發生，A 發生的機率」。

  • Condition: A₁, A₂, … are mutually exclusive and their union is the entire sample space.條件：A₁, A₂, … 互斥且聯集為整個樣本空間。
  • The denominator is the total probability P(B).分母就是全機率 P(B)。

易混淆觀念 32 組

Each group comes with a decision method. On an exam, apply the decision method first, then answer.每一組都附判斷方法。考試看到題目，先套判斷方法再作答。

一、第 1 章：資料分類

1. 元素 vs 變數 vs 觀測值

  • Element = row (entity, e.g., each tablet); variable = column (characteristic, e.g., price); observation = the values of an entire row.元素＝列（對象，例如每台平板）；變數＝欄（特徵，例如價格）；觀測值＝一整列的數值。
  • Trap: the first column of a table, "name/model," is used to identify elements and is not a variable.陷阱：表格第一欄「名稱／型號」是用來辨認元素的，不算變數。
  • Number of elements = number of observations; total data values = elements × variables.元素數＝觀測值數；資料值總數＝元素數 × 變數數。

2. 名目 vs 順序

  • Decision: can the categories be ranked as "better/worse" or "larger/smaller"? Yes = ordinal, no = nominal.判斷：類別之間能不能排「好壞／大小」？能＝順序，不能＝名目。
  • Trap: numbers used as codes (1 = management, 2 = engineering) are still nominal; ranks 1, 2, 3 are ordinal.陷阱：用數字當代碼（1＝管理、2＝工程）仍是名目；名次 1、2、3 是順序。

3. 區間 vs 比率（最常錯）

  • Decision: is 0 "none at all"? Can you say "twice as much"?判斷：0 是不是「完全沒有」？能不能說「兩倍」？
  • 0°C is not "no temperature" → interval; year, SAT score → interval.攝氏溫度 0°C 不是「沒有溫度」→ 區間；年份、SAT 分數 → 區間。
  • Price, weight, time, hours, distance, age, screen size → ratio.價格、重量、時間、時數、距離、年齡、螢幕尺寸 → 比率。
  • Ratings of 0–100 (e.g., the jacket rating in homework problem 4) are usually treated as interval; don't agonize over it if the exam doesn't ask.評分 0～100（如作業第 4 題外套評分）通常視為區間；考試若沒問就不用糾結。

4. 類別資料可以是數字嗎？

  • Yes. Postal codes, student IDs, phone numbers, and codes 1/2/3 are all numbers but are categorical data, because adding, subtracting, or averaging them is meaningless.可以。郵遞區號、學號、電話號碼、代碼 1/2/3 都是數字但屬類別資料，因為加減平均沒有意義。
  • Quantitative data are always numeric; categorical data are not necessarily non-numeric.數量資料一定是數字；類別資料不一定不是數字。

5. 橫斷面 vs 時間序列

  • Same time, many entities → cross-sectional; same entity, many times → time series.同一時間、很多對象 → 橫斷面；同一對象、很多時間 → 時間序列。

6. 觀察研究 vs 實驗研究

  • Does the researcher control the variables? No control = observational (survey, smoking study); control = experimental (RCT).研究者有沒有控制變數？沒控制＝觀察（問卷、吸菸研究）；有控制＝實驗（RCT）。

10. 次數 vs 相對次數 vs 百分比次數 vs 累積次數

  • Frequency: how many. Relative frequency: frequency ÷ n (sums to 1). Percent frequency: × 100 (sums to 100%).次數：有幾個。相對次數：次數 ÷ n（加總 = 1）。百分比次數：× 100（加總 = 100%）。
  • Cumulative frequency: how many up to the class upper limit; the last class has cumulative frequency = n and cumulative relative frequency = 1.累積次數：到該組上限為止共有幾個；最後一組累積次數 = n，累積相對次數 = 1。

11. 莖葉圖 vs 直方圖

  • A stem-and-leaf display keeps the original values and shows order and shape; a histogram shows only grouped frequencies.莖葉圖保留原始數值＋顯示排序與形狀；直方圖只看得到分組次數。
  • Stretched stem-and-leaf: the same stem appears twice, first row leaves 0–4, second row leaves 5–9. Even if a row has no leaves, write out the stem.伸展莖葉圖：同一個莖出現兩次，第一行葉 0～4，第二行葉 5～9。即使某一行沒有葉，也要把莖寫出來。

12. 左偏 vs 右偏（方向看尾巴，不是看高峰）

  • Long tail on the right → right-skewed (positively skewed) → mean > median (income, in-game spending).長尾在右 → 右偏（正偏）→ 平均數 ＞ 中位數（所得、課金）。
  • Long tail on the left → left-skewed (negatively skewed) → mean < median (easy exam).長尾在左 → 左偏（負偏）→ 平均數 ＜ 中位數（簡單考試）。
  • Trap: a graph with its peak on the right is left-skewed.陷阱：高峰在右邊的圖是左偏。

13. 群組長條圖 vs 堆疊長條圖

  • Side-by-side: compare individual differences between groups. Stacked: see totals and composition; with percentage stacking every bar reaches 100%.群組：比較各組個別差異。堆疊：看總量與組成；百分比堆疊時每條都到 100%。

14. 交叉表 vs 散佈圖

  • Crosstabulation: any combination (categorical/quantitative) works; quantitative variables must be grouped first.交叉表：任何組合（類別／數量）都可，數量變數要先分組。
  • Scatter diagram: both must be quantitative variables.散佈圖：兩個都要是數量變數。

15. 辛普森悖論

  • The conclusion from grouped data is the opposite of the aggregated one. If the "aggregated table" and "grouped tables" give different conclusions, this is it; the cause is a lurking variable or very different sample sizes across groups.分組後的結論與合併後相反。看到「合併表」與「分組表」結論不同就是它；原因是有隱藏變數，或各組樣本數差很多。

三、第 3 章：數值量數

16. 平均數 vs 中位數 vs 眾數

  • With extreme values → use the median (income, house prices).有極端值 → 用中位數（所得、房價）。
  • For categorical data, only the mode can be used.類別資料只能用眾數。
  • The median is not necessarily a value in the data (with an even number of values, take the average).中位數不一定是資料中的某個值（偶數筆時取平均）。

18. 中位數位置 (n+1)/2 vs 百分位位置 (p/100)(n+1)

  • In this textbook the two agree: the median is the percentile at p = 50.本課本兩者一致：中位數就是 p = 50 的百分位數。
  • Position has a decimal → use interpolation: i-th value + decimal × ((i+1)-th − i-th).位置有小數 → 用內插：第 i 個 ＋ 小數 ×（第 i+1 個 − 第 i 個）。
  • Trap: you must sort first; the position is "which one," not the value itself.陷阱：一定要先排序；位置是「第幾個」，不是值本身。
  • Note: some textbooks use the L = (p/100)·n version, which gives slightly different answers. On the exam, follow the instructor's (n+1) version.注意：有些課本用 L = (p/100)·n 的版本，答案會略不同。考試請照老師講義的 (n+1) 版本。

19. 樣本變異數 vs 母體變異數

  • Sample ÷ (n − 1), population ÷ N. If the problem says "a sample of …", use n − 1.樣本 ÷ (n − 1)，母體 ÷ N。題目說「a sample of …」就用 n − 1。
  • Standard deviation = √variance; variance is in "squared" units, while the standard deviation has the same units as the original data.標準差 = √變異數；變異數單位是「平方」，標準差單位跟原資料一樣。

20. 標準差 vs 變異係數

  • Standard deviation: absolute variability. CV: relative variability (÷ mean).標準差：絕對變異。CV：相對變異（÷ 平均數）。
  • Means differ greatly or units differ → compare CV, not standard deviation.平均數差很多或單位不同 → 比較 CV，不要比標準差。

21. 全距 vs IQR

  • Range uses the maximum and minimum → vulnerable to extreme values; IQR looks only at the middle 50% → not affected by extreme values.全距用最大、最小值 → 怕極端值；IQR 只看中間 50% → 不怕極端值。

  • Covariance shows only direction, and its size depends on units; the correlation coefficient lies between −1 and +1 and allows comparing strength.共變異數只看方向，大小受單位影響；相關係數在 −1～+1，可以比較強弱。

  • Correlation ≠ causation.相關 ≠ 因果。

25. 組合 vs 排列

  • Order does not matter (choosing a committee, sampling) → combination C(N, n).順序不重要（選委員、抽樣本）→ 組合 C(N, n)。

26. 古典法 vs 相對次數法 vs 主觀法

  • Equally likely → classical; proportions from historical data → relative frequency; expert judgment → subjective.等可能 → 古典；用歷史資料算比例 → 相對次數；專家判斷 → 主觀。

看參考答案

1) 工時 50 小時的 z 分數　z = 2

  • z = (50 − 40) / 5 = 2
  • 意義：這位員工的工時比平均多 2 個標準差，高於平均。
ENGLISH ANSWER — 可直接寫在作業上

z = (50 − 40) / 5 = 2. The employee’s weekly working time is 2 standard deviations above the mean, so this employee works considerably more hours than the average employee.

中文z = (50 − 40) / 5 = 2。這位員工的每週工時比平均數高 2 個標準差，工作時數明顯比一般員工多。

8-2According to Chebyshev’s Theorem, what is the minimum percentage of employees who work between 30 and 50 hours per week? (2 points)

看參考答案

2) 工時介於 30～50 小時的最少比例　至少 75%

  • 30 的 z = (30 − 40)/5 = −2；50 的 z = +2 → 範圍是平均數 ± 2 個標準差。

Q9 16%Suppose there is a .02 probability that a male patient has prostate cancer before testing. The probability of a false-positive PSA test is .75, and the probability of a false-negative is .20.

9-1What is the probability that the male patient has prostate cancer if the PSA test comes back positive? (4 points)

看參考答案

1) 檢驗陽性時有癌症的機率 P(C｜+)　≈ 0.0213

先把題目翻成符號

  • C = 有攝護腺癌；N = 沒有癌症；+ = 檢驗陽性；− = 檢驗陰性
  • 事前：P(C) = 0.02 → P(N) = 0.98
  • 偽陽性（沒病卻陽性）：P(+｜N) = 0.75 → P(−｜N) = 0.25
  • 偽陰性（有病卻陰性）：P(−｜C) = 0.20 → P(+｜C) = 0.80
事件 事前 P 條件 P(+｜·) 聯合 P(· ∩ +) 事後 P(·｜+)
C 0.02 0.80 0.016 0.016/0.751 = 0.0213
N 0.98 0.75 0.735 0.9787
合計 1.00 P(+) = 0.751 1.0000

解讀：驗出陽性後，有癌症的機率只從 2% 升到約 2.13%。因為偽陽性率高達 75%，陽性結果幾乎提供不了資訊。

ENGLISH ANSWER — 可直接寫在作業上

P(C | +) = (0.02)(0.80) / [(0.02)(0.80) + (0.98)(0.75)] = 0.016 / 0.751 ≈ 0.0213. If the PSA test comes back positive, the probability that the patient has prostate cancer is only about 0.0213. Because the false-positive rate is very high (0.75), a positive result increases the probability of cancer only slightly from the prior probability of 0.02.

中文若 PSA 檢驗結果為陽性，病人罹患攝護腺癌的機率只有約 0.0213。因為偽陽性率（false-positive rate）非常高（0.75），陽性結果只讓罹癌機率從事前機率 0.02 稍微上升。

事件 事前 P 條件 P(−｜·) 聯合 P(· ∩ −) 事後 P(·｜−)
C 0.02 0.20 0.004 0.004/0.249 = 0.0161
N 0.98 0.25 0.245 0.9839
合計 1.00 P(−) = 0.249 1.0000

ENGLISH ANSWER — 可直接寫在作業上

P(C | −) = (0.02)(0.20) / [(0.02)(0.20) + (0.98)(0.25)] = 0.004 / 0.249 ≈ 0.0161. If the PSA test comes back negative, the probability that the patient has prostate cancer is about 0.0161, only slightly lower than the prior probability of 0.02.

中文若 PSA 檢驗結果為陰性，病人罹癌的機率約 0.0161，只比事前機率 0.02 稍低一點。

看參考答案

3) 事前機率改為 0.4

事件 事前 P 條件 P(−｜·) 聯合 事後

Ch1 Data and Statistics

1.1

什麼是統計

  • Statistics as data (numerical facts): mean, median, percentages; statistics as a discipline: methods for collecting, analyzing, presenting, and interpreting data (art and science).統計數據（numerical facts）：平均數、中位數、百分比；統計學：蒐集、分析、呈現、解釋資料的方法（art and science）。
1.2

資料的基本名詞（期中必考，作業第 1 題）

Mnemonic口訣
  • "Elements are rows, variables are columns, an observation is a whole row"「元素是列、變數是欄、觀測值是一整列」
  • data set the whole table; element the entity data are collected on (each student); variable a characteristic (age, major); observation the results of one element on all variables.data set 整張表；element 被蒐集的對象（每位學生）；variable 特徵（年齡、主修）；observation 某一元素在所有變數上的結果。
  • n elements → n observations; total data values = elements × variables.n 個元素 → n 個觀測值；資料值總數 = 元素數 × 變數數。
  • When counting variables, the name column (e.g., tablet model) does not count; the header row is not an element.數變數時，名稱欄（例如平板型號）不算變數；表頭不算元素。
1.3

衡量尺度（Scales of Measurement）

Mnemonic口訣
  • "Nominal, Ordinal, Interval, Ratio": each level has the properties of the previous one「名、序、區、比」，每一層都有前一層的性質
  • Nominal is just labels (postal code, operating system); Ordinal has order but distances are meaningless (satisfaction, rank); Interval has meaningful distances, no true zero (Celsius, SAT, year); Ratio has a true zero (price, time, battery life).Nominal 只是標籤（郵遞區號、作業系統）；Ordinal 有順序但間距沒意義（滿意度、名次）；Interval 間距有意義、沒有真正的 0（攝氏、SAT、年份）；Ratio 有真正的 0（價格、時間、續航）。
  • Decision: label → is there an order? Numeric → does 0 mean none at all? Can say "twice" → ratio; 20°C is not twice as hot as 10°C → interval.判斷：是標籤 → 有順序？是數值 → 0 代表完全沒有嗎？能說「兩倍」→ 比率；20°C 不是 10°C 的兩倍熱 → 區間。
1.4

類別資料 vs 數量資料

  • Categorical data (qualitative): nominal or ordinal; can be numeric codes, but arithmetic is meaningless (codes cannot be averaged).類別資料（qualitative）：名目或順序，可以是數字代碼，但算術沒意義（代碼不能平均）。
  • Quantitative data: interval or ratio; always numeric, arithmetic is allowed, more analysis methods.數量資料：區間或比率，一定是數字，可以做算術，分析方法較多。
1.5

橫斷面資料 vs 時間序列資料

  • Cross-sectional: many entities at the same time (building permits by county/city in September 2025). Time series: one entity over many times (Taipei's 36 months of building permits), for trends and forecasting.橫斷面：同一時間、多個對象（2025 年 9 月各縣市建照數）。時間序列：同一對象、多個時間點（台北 36 個月建照數），看趨勢、預測。
1.6

資料來源

  • Existing data: internal records, commercial databases, government, trade associations, the internet.既有資料：內部紀錄、商業資料庫、政府、公會、網路。
  • Observational study: variables not controlled (survey, smokers vs non-smokers); experimental study: other variables controlled (RCT). Considerations: time, cost, data errors.觀察研究：不控制變數（問卷、吸菸者 vs 非吸菸者）；實驗研究：控制其他變數（RCT）。考量：時間、成本、資料錯誤。
1.7

敘述統計 vs 推論統計

  • Descriptive statistics: summarize data with tables, graphs, and numerical measures. Statistical inference: use a sample to estimate population characteristics or test hypotheses.敘述統計：用表、圖、數值摘要資料。統計推論：用樣本估計母體特徵或檢定假設。
  • Population vs sample; census vs sample survey.母體 population vs 樣本 sample；普查 census vs 抽樣調查 sample survey。
1.8

Analytics（分析學）

  • Descriptive (what happened) → predictive (model to predict the future) → prescriptive (recommend the best action).描述性（過去發生什麼）→ 預測性（建模預測未來）→ 處方性（給最佳行動方案）。

Ch2 表格與圖形

2.1

類別資料的整理

  • Relative frequency = frequency ÷ total; percent = relative frequency × 100. Counts alone mislead: A with 10 of 20 (50%) is better than B with 50 of 200 (25%).相對次數 = 次數 ÷ 總數；百分比 = 相對次數 × 100。只看次數會誤判：A 20 則中 10 則（50%）比 B 200 則中 50 則（25%）好。
  • Bar chart: categorical data, bars separated. Pie chart: relative frequency × 360° (0.25 → 90°), suited to few categories and parts of a whole.長條圖：類別資料、長條分開。圓餅圖：相對次數 × 360°（0.25 → 90°），適合類別少、看部分占整體。
2.2

數量資料的整理

Mnemonic口訣
  • Three steps: "classes 5–20 → width ≈ (max − min) ÷ classes, rounded → limits non-overlapping, covering everything"三步驟：「組數 5～20 → 組距 ≈ (最大 − 最小) ÷ 組數取整 → 組限不重疊、全涵蓋」
  • Hudson: (109 − 52) ÷ 6 = 9.5 → class width 10. Cumulative frequency accumulates by class upper limit (≤59, ≤69…); cumulative relative frequency = cumulative frequency ÷ n.Hudson：(109 − 52) ÷ 6 = 9.5 → 組距取 10。累積次數以各組上限累加（≤59、≤69…），累積相對次數 = 累積次數 ÷ n。
  • Histogram bars touch; dot plots and stem-and-leaf displays keep the original data.直方圖長條相連；點圖、莖葉圖保留原始資料。
  • Skewness follows the tail: right-skewed (income, spending) mean > median; left-skewed (easy exam) mean < median; symmetric equal.偏態看尾巴：右偏（所得、課金）平均 > 中位；左偏（簡單考試）平均 < 中位；對稱相等。
  • Stem-and-leaf leaf unit: 1682 with leaf unit 10 → stem 16, leaf 8 (truncate, not round); a stretched display writes each stem twice (0–4, 5–9).莖葉圖葉單位：1682 用葉單位 10 → 莖 16、葉 8（捨去不是四捨五入）；伸展莖葉圖每個莖寫兩次（0～4、5～9）。
2.3

兩個變數：交叉表（Crosstabulation）

  • Crosstabulation: categorical + categorical, categorical + quantitative, quantitative + quantitative (quantitative must be grouped first).交叉表：類別＋類別、類別＋數量、數量＋數量（數量要先分組）。
  • Simpson's paradox: the grouped conclusion reverses after aggregation, caused by a lurking variable (very different group sizes) → look at the grouped tables first.辛普森悖論：分組的結論在合併後反轉，原因是隱藏變數（各組樣本數差很多）→ 先看分組表。
2.4

兩個變數：圖形

  • Scatter diagram + trendline: two quantitative variables (positive, negative, no relationship).散佈圖＋趨勢線：兩個數量變數（正、負、無關係）。
  • Side-by-side bar chart: compare groups; stacked bar chart: see totals and composition; with percentages every bar is equal in height at 100%.群組長條圖：比較各組差異；堆疊長條圖：看總量與組成，用百分比時每條都等高到 100%。
2.5

圖形選擇總表

  • Distribution: categorical → bar, pie; quantitative → dot plot, histogram, stem-and-leaf. Comparison → side-by-side/stacked bars. Relationship → scatter + trendline.分布：類別 → 長條、圓餅；數量 → 點圖、直方圖、莖葉圖。比較 → 群組／堆疊長條圖。關係 → 散佈圖＋趨勢線。
  • Dashboard: shows KPIs; minimize scrolling, no unnecessary color or 3D, separate with borders.Dashboard：呈現 KPI；減少捲動、不要多餘顏色或 3D、用框線分隔。

Ch3 數值量數

3.1

位置量數（Measures of Location）

Mnemonic口訣
  • Percentile: "sort → L = (p/100)(n+1) → integer: take it directly, decimal: interpolate"百分位數：「排序 → L = (p/100)(n+1) → 整數直接取，有小數就內插」
  • The mean is strongly affected by extreme values; with extreme values (income, house prices) use the median; the mode can be bimodal or multimodal.平均數受極端值影響大；有極端值（所得、房價）用中位數；眾數可以雙峰、多峰。
  • Weighted mean Σwx ÷ Σw (GPA, average wage: directly averaging 5 wages is wrong).加權平均 Σwx ÷ Σw（GPA、平均工資：5 種工資直接平均是錯的）。
  • Geometric mean: convert returns to 1 + r (−10% → 0.90), multiply all, take the nth root, then −1.幾何平均：報酬率換成 1 + r（−10% → 0.90），全部相乘開 n 次方，再 −1。
  • 80th percentile of 70 rents: L = 0.8 × 71 = 56.8 → 635 + 0.8(649 − 635) = 646.2. Q1, Q2, Q3 = 25th, 50th, 75th percentiles.70 筆租金 80th：L = 0.8 × 71 = 56.8 → 635 + 0.8(649 − 635) = 646.2。Q1、Q2、Q3 = 第 25、50、75 百分位數。
3.2

離散量數（Measures of Variability）

  • Range = max − min (extremely sensitive to extreme values); IQR = Q3 − Q1 (not affected by extreme values).全距 = 最大 − 最小（對極端值超敏感）；IQR = Q3 − Q1（不受極端值影響）。
  • Sample variance s² has denominator n − 1 (using x̄ underestimates variability); population σ² has denominator N; standard deviation has the same units as the data.樣本變異數 s² 分母 n − 1（用 x̄ 會低估變異）；母體 σ² 分母 N；標準差單位和原資料相同。
  • CV = s / x̄ × 100%, for comparison across units: A (100, 10) 10%, B (1000, 50) 5% → B has smaller relative variability.CV = s / x̄ × 100%，跨單位比較：A（100, 10）10%、B（1000, 50）5% → B 相對變異較小。
3.3

分布形狀、相對位置、離群值

Mnemonic口訣
  • "Direction follows the tail; the mean runs with the tail"「方向看尾巴，平均數跟著尾巴跑」
  • z = (x − x̄) / s: how many standard deviations from the mean.z = (x − x̄) / s：離平均幾個標準差。
  • Chebyshev (any distribution): at least 1 − 1/z²: z = 2 → 75%, z = 3 → 88.89%, z = 4 → 93.75%.柴比雪夫（任何分布）：至少 1 − 1/z²：z = 2 → 75%、z = 3 → 88.89%、z = 4 → 93.75%。
  • Empirical rule (bell-shaped only): 68%, 95%, 99.7%. If the distribution is unknown, only Chebyshev applies.經驗法則（只有鐘形）：68%、95%、99.7%。分布未知只能用柴比雪夫。
  • Outliers: |z| > 3, or beyond Q1 − 1.5·IQR, Q3 + 1.5·IQR; not necessarily deleted.離群值：|z| > 3，或超出 Q1 − 1.5·IQR、Q3 + 1.5·IQR；不一定要刪。
3.4

五數摘要與盒鬚圖

  • Five-number summary: minimum, Q1, median, Q3, maximum.五數摘要：最小、Q1、中位數、Q3、最大。
  • Box plot: box spans Q1 to Q3, the line inside is the median; limits only locate and are not drawn; whiskers reach the smallest and largest values within the limits; outliers marked *.盒鬚圖：盒子 Q1～Q3、中間一線是中位數；上下限只定位不畫；鬚線到限內的最小最大值；離群值標 *。
  • Rent: Q1 545, Q3 625, IQR 80 → lower limit 425, upper limit 745.租金：Q1 545、Q3 625、IQR 80 → 下限 425、上限 745。
3.5

兩變數的關聯

  • Covariance sxy = Σ(x − x̄)(y − ȳ) / (n − 1): shows direction only, affected by units.共變異數 sxy = Σ(x − x̄)(y − ȳ) / (n − 1)：只看方向，受單位影響。
  • Correlation coefficient r = sxy / (sx·sy): −1 to +1, unitless, shows direction and strength.相關係數 r = sxy / (sx·sy)：−1～+1、無單位，看方向和強度。
  • Measures only linear relationships; correlation does not imply causation. Golf: the longer the distance, the lower the strokes → r ≈ −0.96.只衡量線性關係；相關不等於因果。高爾夫：距離越遠桿數越低 → r ≈ −0.96。

Ch4 機率導論

4.1

隨機實驗、樣本空間、計數法則

  • Sample space S: all experimental outcomes; each outcome is a sample point.樣本空間 S：所有實驗結果；每個結果是樣本點。
  • Multiple-step counting: n₁ × n₂ × … (Bradley 4 × 2 = 8, use a tree diagram).多步驟計數：n₁ × n₂ × …（Bradley 4 × 2 = 8，用樹狀圖）。
  • Combination (order does not matter) C(N, n) = N! / [n!(N − n)!]; permutation (order matters) P(N, n) = N! / (N − n)!; P = n! × C; 0! = 1.組合（順序不重要）C(N, n) = N! / [n!(N − n)!]；排列（順序重要）P(N, n) = N! / (N − n)!；P = n! × C；0! = 1。
4.2

指派機率

  • Two requirements: 0 ≤ P ≤ 1, and all probabilities sum to 1.兩個要求：0 ≤ P ≤ 1、全部加起來 = 1。
  • Classical (equally likely, die 1/6), relative frequency (Lucas: 18 of 40 days → 0.45), subjective (expert judgment).古典法（等可能，骰子 1/6）、相對次數法（Lucas：40 天中 18 天 → 0.45）、主觀法（專家判斷）。
  • Event probability = sum of the probabilities of the sample points it contains.事件機率 = 它所含樣本點機率加總。
4.3

基本機率關係

  • Complement P(Aᶜ) = 1 − P(A).補集 P(Aᶜ) = 1 − P(A)。
  • Addition law P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A ∩ B); Bradley: 0.70 + 0.48 − 0.36 = 0.82.加法律 P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A ∩ B)；Bradley：0.70 + 0.48 − 0.36 = 0.82。
  • Mutually exclusive → P(A ∩ B) = 0 → just add. Multiplication law P(A ∩ B) = P(B)·P(A|B).互斥 → P(A ∩ B) = 0 → 直接相加。乘法律 P(A ∩ B) = P(B)·P(A｜B)。
4.4

條件機率與獨立

  • P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B); from a joint probability table: that cell ÷ the total of B's row (column).P(A｜B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)；從聯合機率表算：該格 ÷ B 那一列（欄）的合計。
  • The interior holds joint probabilities, the margins hold marginal probabilities.表內是聯合機率，邊緣是邊際機率。
  • Independent: P(A|B) = P(A), equivalent to P(A ∩ B) = P(A)P(B).獨立：P(A｜B) = P(A)，等價 P(A ∩ B) = P(A)P(B)。
  • Mutually exclusive ≠ independent: mutually exclusive events with both probabilities > 0 are definitely not independent.互斥 ≠ 獨立：兩個機率都 > 0 的互斥事件一定不獨立。
4.5

貝氏定理（Bayes’ Theorem）

Mnemonic口訣
  • Five table columns: "Events, prior, conditional, joint = prior × conditional, posterior = joint ÷ total"表格五欄：「事件、事前、條件、聯合 = 事前 × 條件、事後 = 聯合 ÷ 合計」
  • Prior P(Aᵢ) → new information B → posterior P(Aᵢ|B); the denominator is the total probability P(B); the Aᵢ must be mutually exclusive and their union everything.事前 P(Aᵢ) → 新資訊 B → 事後 P(Aᵢ｜B)；分母是全機率 P(B)；Aᵢ 必須互斥且聯集為全部。
  • Example: 0.7 × 0.2 = 0.14, 0.3 × 0.9 = 0.27 → P(B) = 0.41 → posteriors 0.3415, 0.6585. The conditional column need not sum to 1.例：0.7 × 0.2 = 0.14、0.3 × 0.9 = 0.27 → P(B) = 0.41 → 事後 0.3415、0.6585。條件那一欄加起來不必是 1。
