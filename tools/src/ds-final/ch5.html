What a queue is Slide 3什麼是佇列

A queue is a linear list in which data can only be inserted at one end, called the rear, and deleted from the other end, called the front. It is a first in, first out (FIFO) structure.佇列是一種線性串列，資料只能從一端（rear，尾端）插入、從另一端（front，前端）刪除。它是先進先出（FIFO）的結構。

In plain wordsA queue is a line at a ticket window: newcomers join at the back, and the person at the front is served first. Compare with Ch4: a stack has one end (top, LIFO); a queue has two ends (front and rear, FIFO).白話 佇列就是排隊買票：新來的排在最後面，最前面的人先被服務。和 Ch4 比：stack 只有一端（top，後進先出）；queue 有兩端（front 和 rear，先進先出）。

5-1 Queue Operations ★★★佇列的運算

Four basic operations: data can be inserted at the rear, deleted from the front, retrieved from the front, retrieved from the rear. The queue needs to keep track of the front and the rear; the stack only needs to worry about one end, the top.四個基本運算：從 rear 插入、從 front 刪除、取 front 的資料、取 rear 的資料。佇列要同時記住 front 和 rear；stack 只要管一端（top）。

Algorithm 5-2enqueueSlide 23 to 28入列

A little more complex than inserting data into a stack: we analyze three conditions: insertion into an empty queue, into a queue with data, and when there is no memory.比 stack 的插入複雜一點，要分析三種情況：插入空佇列、插入有資料的佇列、沒有記憶體時插入。

  • Empty queue: front and rear pointers must point to the new node.空佇列：front 和 rear 都要指向新節點。
  • Queue with data: two pointers must point to the new node: the next pointer in the last node and the rear pointer in the head node.有資料的佇列：兩個指標要指向新節點：最後一個節點的 next 和 head 的 rear。

5-3 Queuing Theory ★★等候理論

  • Queuing theory is a field of applied mathematics that is used to predict the performance of queues.等候理論是應用數學的一個領域，用來預測佇列的效能。
  • Queues are divided into single-server (service to only one customer at a time) and multiserver (service to many customers at a time).佇列分成單一服務者（一次只服務一位顧客）和多服務者（一次服務多位）。
  • A customer is any person or thing needing service; a service is any activity needed to accomplish the required result.顧客是任何需要服務的人或東西；服務是完成需求所需的任何活動。
  • Two factors that most affect the queue: the arrival rate (the rate at which customers arrive; it may be random or regular) and the service time (the average time required to complete the processing of a customer request).影響佇列最大的兩個因素：到達率（顧客進入佇列的速率，可能隨機或規律）和服務時間（處理一位顧客平均需要的時間）。
  • Queue length: the queue is longer when customers arrive faster and the service time is higher. Ideally customers arrive at a rate that matches the service time; in reality the server is sometimes idle and sometimes many customers wait.佇列長度：顧客來得越快、服務時間越長，佇列越長。理想狀況是到達速率剛好配合服務時間；實際上伺服器有時閒置、有時很多人在等。
  • Queuing theory tries to minimize idle servers and waiting customers. It predicts queue time, the average size of the queue and the maximum queue size, and to do so it needs two factors: the arrival rate and the average service time.等候理論想讓閒置的伺服器和等待的顧客都最少。它能預測排隊時間、平均佇列長度和最大佇列長度，需要兩個因素：到達率和平均服務時間。
  • Response time: the average time from when customers enter the queue until they leave the server. Response time = queue time + service time. A model can then study proposed changes (e.g. if we reduce the average service time by 15%, how many fewer tellers would we need?).回應時間：從顧客進入佇列到離開服務者的平均時間。回應時間 = 排隊時間 + 服務時間。建好模型後可以拿來研究改變（例如服務時間減少 15%，可以少幾個櫃員？）。

5-4 Queue Applications ★★★佇列的應用

Queues are one of the most common data structures, used in operating systems, networks and countless other areas. Two simple applications: queue simulation (can be used to study the performance of any queue application) and categorizing data.佇列是最常見的資料結構之一，用在作業系統、網路等許多領域。兩個簡單的應用：佇列模擬（可研究任何佇列應用的效能）和資料分類。

SimulationTaffy store modelSlide 46 to 59模擬的設定與資料結構

A modeling activity to generate statistics about the performance of queues. Example: a taffy store with one window and one clerk who can service only one customer at a time (a single-server queue). Mailing boxes takes longer, so service time varies between 1 and 10 minutes. The store is open 8 hours a day, so one simulated day is 480 minutes (8 × 60).模擬是一種建模活動，用來產生佇列效能的統計。例子：一家只有一個窗口、一位店員的太妃糖店，一次只能服務一位顧客（單一服務者佇列）。郵寄比較花時間，所以服務時間在 1 到 10 分鐘之間。店一天開 8 小時，所以模擬一天是 480 分鐘。

  • The simulation uses a digital clock: events start and stop in 1-minute intervals; customers arrive on the minute and wait and are served an integral number of minutes.模擬用數位時鐘：事件以 1 分鐘為單位開始和結束；顧客整分鐘到達，等待和服務時間也都是整數分鐘。
  • Each minute it checks three events: the arrival of customers (new customer), server free (start customer processing), complete customer processing (service complete).每分鐘檢查三個事件：顧客到達（new customer）、服務者空閒（開始服務）、服務完成。
  • New customer: a random number generator returns 1 to 4; if it is 4 a customer has arrived; 1, 2, 3 mean no arrival.新顧客：亂數產生 1 到 4；是 4 就代表有顧客到達，1、2、3 代表沒有。
  • Server free: if the clerk is idle, the next waiting customer is served; if busy, the waiting customers stay in the queue.服務者空閒：店員閒著就服務下一位排隊的顧客；忙的話大家繼續排隊。
  • Completed processing: at the end of each minute, check whether the current customer is done (service time from a random number); if so, gather statistics and set the server to idle.服務完成：每分鐘結束時檢查目前顧客是否完成（服務時間由亂數決定）；完成就收集統計並把服務者設成閒置。

Analysis: the driver creates the queue and loops until the simulation is complete; each loop checks newCustomer, serverFree, svcComplete. The simulation is complete only when both we have run for the allocated time and there are no more customers. The flag moreCusts tracks this: serverFree sets it true when it starts a call (5.2), svcComplete sets it false when it completes a call (5.3), and the driver sets it true if calls are waiting in the queue (5.4). At the end a print algorithm calculates the averages and prints all the statistics.分析：主程式建立佇列後一直迴圈，每圈檢查 newCustomer、serverFree、svcComplete。要「時間跑完」而且「沒有顧客了」才算結束。旗標 moreCusts 負責記錄：serverFree 開始服務時設 true（5.2），svcComplete 完成時設 false（5.3），佇列還有人在等時主程式再設 true（5.4）。最後印出統計。

Chapter summarySummary 本章總結
  • A queue inserts at the rear and deletes at the front: FIFO. Operations: enqueue (overflow), dequeue (underflow), queue front, queue rear.佇列從 rear 插入、從 front 刪除：先進先出。運算：enqueue（overflow）、dequeue（underflow）、queue front、queue rear。
  • Linked-list queue: a head with front, count, rear and nodes with data and next. Eight algorithms: create, enqueue, dequeue, queue front/rear, empty, full, count, destroy.鏈結串列佇列：head 有 front、count、rear，節點有 data 和 next。八個演算法：create、enqueue、dequeue、queue front／rear、empty、full、count、destroy。
  • Enqueue into an empty queue sets front and rear; into a queue with data sets rear->next and rear. Dequeuing the only item also sets rear to null.enqueue 到空佇列要設 front 和 rear；有資料時設 rear->next 和 rear。dequeue 唯一元素時 rear 也要設成 null。
  • Queuing theory predicts queue time, average and maximum queue size from the arrival rate and the service time; response time = queue time + service time.等候理論用到達率和服務時間預測排隊時間、平均與最大佇列長度；回應時間 = 排隊時間 + 服務時間。
  • Taffy simulation: 480 minutes, one arrival when (random mod 4) + 1 = 4, service 1 to 10 minutes, end of service = start + service − 1, wait = start − arrival.太妃糖模擬：480 分鐘，(亂數 mod 4) + 1 = 4 時有人到，服務 1 到 10 分鐘，結束時間 = 開始 + 服務 − 1，等待 = 開始 − 到達。
  • Categorizing: one queue per category keeps each group in its original order; the result is not sorted.資料分類：每類一個佇列，各組維持原本順序；結果不是排序。
Common trap易錯點
  • Enqueue at the rear, dequeue at the front, not the other way round. A stack needs one end; a queue needs two pointers.從 rear enqueue、從 front dequeue，不要弄反。stack 只要一端；queue 要兩個指標。
  • enqueue tests an empty queue with queue.count zero (front null or rear null would also work); line 9 queue.rear = newPtr runs in both cases.enqueue 用 queue.count zero 判斷空佇列（用 front 或 rear 是 null 也可以）；第 9 行 queue.rear = newPtr 兩種情況都會跑。
  • dequeue of the last item: forgetting queue.rear = null leaves rear pointing to a recycled node.dequeue 最後一個元素時忘了 queue.rear = null，rear 會指向已回收的節點。
  • fullQueue works by allocating and then recycling a node, not by comparing the count to a maximum.fullQueue 是靠「配置再回收」一個節點判斷，不是拿 count 跟最大值比。
  • End of service is start + service − 1, not start + service. The simulation loop is clock <= endTime OR moreCusts.服務結束是開始 + 服務 − 1，不是開始 + 服務。模擬迴圈是 clock <= endTime OR moreCusts。
  • Response time = queue time + service time; queuing theory needs the arrival rate and the average service time.回應時間 = 排隊時間 + 服務時間；等候理論需要到達率和平均服務時間。
  • Categorizing does not sort: 3 6 9 4 5 stays in input order inside its group.分類不會排序：3 6 9 4 5 在組內維持輸入的順序。
