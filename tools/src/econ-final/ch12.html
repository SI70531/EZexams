A good tax system is judged on two goals. Efficiency: a tax costs more than the money collected, because it creates a deadweight loss (people change what they do) and an administrative burden (time and money spent complying). The marginal tax rate drives incentives; a lump-sum tax has a marginal rate of 0 and is the most efficient. Equity: pay by the benefits you receive, or by your ability to pay (vertical and horizontal equity). The two goals often conflict.好的稅制看兩個目標。效率：稅的成本比收到的錢多，因為有無謂損失（人們改變行為）和行政負擔（報稅花的時間和錢）。影響誘因的是邊際稅率；定額稅的邊際稅率是 0，效率最高。公平：依受益多寡繳，或依負擔能力繳（垂直公平、水平公平）。兩個目標常常互相衝突。

★★★ Taxes and efficiency: the costs of a tax稅與效率：課稅的成本

Policymakers care about two goals: efficiency and equity. One tax system is more efficient than another if it raises the same revenue at a smaller cost to taxpayers. The costs of taxes to taxpayers:政策制定者在乎兩個目標：效率和公平。收到一樣多的稅、但納稅人成本較低的稅制比較有效率。納稅人負擔的成本有：

Cost成本What it is是什麼
1. The tax payment itself1. 稅款本身A transfer from the taxpayer to the government從納稅人移轉到政府的錢
2. Deadweight loss2. 無謂損失Taxes distort the decisions people make: the fall in taxpayers' well-being exceeds the revenue the government collects稅扭曲人們的決策：納稅人福利的減少超過政府收到的稅
3. Administrative burden3. 行政負擔What taxpayers bear as they comply with the tax laws納稅人為了遵守稅法所承擔的負擔
  • "People respond to incentives": taxes distort incentives, so people allocate resources according to tax incentives rather than true costs and benefits.「人們會對誘因做出反應」：稅扭曲誘因，人們依照稅的誘因、而不是真正的成本和效益來分配資源。
Example (p14): a tax on pizzaThe price of pizza is $5. Jack values a pizza at $8, Jane at $6. The government levies a $2 tax on pizza and the price rises to $7. Find the total surplus before and after the tax, and the deadweight loss.例子（p14）：披薩稅 披薩一個 $5。Jack 覺得值 $8，Jane 覺得值 $6。政府每個課 $2，價格漲到 $7。求課稅前後的總剩餘和無謂損失。
看解答

Before: Jack's surplus = 8 − 5 = $3, Jane's = 6 − 5 = $1, total surplus = $4.
After: Jack still buys, surplus = 8 − 7 = $1. Jane (value $6 < $7) stops buying. The government collects $2 on Jack's pizza. Total surplus = 1 + 2 = $3.
Deadweight loss = 4 − 3 = $1: Jane's lost surplus, which nobody gets.課稅前：Jack 剩餘 3、Jane 剩餘 1，總剩餘 4。課稅後：Jack 還買，剩餘 1；Jane 不買了；政府從 Jack 收 2。總剩餘 = 1 + 2 = 3。無謂損失 = 4 − 3 = 1，就是 Jane 消失的剩餘，沒有任何人拿到。作業第 3 題（擊劍課）是同樣的算法。

★★ Administrative burden行政負擔

  • Includes the time and money people spend to comply with tax laws (keeping records, filling in forms).包括人們為了遵守稅法花的時間和金錢（保存紀錄、填表）。
  • Encourages spending resources on legal tax avoidance, e.g. hiring accountants to exploit "loopholes" to reduce one's tax burden.鼓勵人們花資源做合法節稅，例如請會計師利用「漏洞」降低稅負。
  • It is a type of deadweight loss: resources are used up and nobody gets them.它是一種無謂損失：資源被用掉了，沒有任何人得到。
  • It could be reduced if the tax code were simplified, but that would require removing loopholes, which is politically difficult.把稅法簡化可以降低行政負擔，但要拿掉漏洞，政治上很難。

★★★ Should income or consumption be taxed?該對所得課稅，還是對消費課稅？

  • An income tax reduces the incentive to save: with a 25% income tax rate, an 8% interest rate becomes a 6% after-tax interest rate, and the lost income compounds over time.所得稅降低儲蓄誘因：所得稅率 25% 時，8% 的利率稅後只剩 6%，少掉的收入還會複利累積。
  • Some economists advocate taxing consumption instead: this would restore the incentive to save, which is better for people's retirement income security and long-run economic growth.有些經濟學家主張改課消費：可以恢復儲蓄誘因，對退休生活保障和長期經濟成長都比較好。
  • Consumption-tax-like provisions in the U.S. tax code: Individual Retirement Accounts (IRA) and 401(k) plans. People can put a limited amount of saving into them, and the funds are not taxed until withdrawn at retirement. So they make the U.S. system more like a consumption tax.美國稅法中像消費稅的設計：個人退休帳戶（IRA）和 401(k)。存進去的錢（有上限）退休提領前不課稅，所以讓美國稅制更像消費稅。
  • Europe's value-added tax (VAT, 增值稅／加值型營業稅) is like a consumption tax.歐洲的加值稅（VAT）就像消費稅。

★★★ Taxes and equity: the benefits principle and the ability-to-pay principle稅與公平：受益原則與量能課稅原則

Equity means distributing the burden of taxes "fairly". Agreeing on what is fair is much harder than agreeing on what is efficient, but there are several principles people use.公平是把稅的負擔「公平地」分配。要對什麼是公平達成共識，比對效率難得多，但有幾個大家常用的原則。

Principle原則Idea想法Example例子
Benefits principle受益原則People should pay taxes based on the benefits they receive from government services. Tries to make public goods similar to private goods (用多少付多少).依照從政府服務得到的好處繳稅，讓公共財像私有財一樣：用多少付多少。Gasoline taxes: the tax paid is related to how much a person uses public roads.汽油稅：繳多少和用多少公共道路有關。
Ability-to-pay principle量能課稅原則Taxes should be levied according to how well a person can shoulder the burden; all taxpayers should make an "equal sacrifice".依照每個人承擔負擔的能力課稅，讓每個人做「同等的犧牲」。A $10,000 tax bill is a bigger sacrifice for a poor person than for a rich person.$10,000 的稅對窮人是比對富人更大的犧牲。

The ability-to-pay principle leads to two ideas of equity:量能課稅原則衍生出兩種公平：

  • Vertical equity: taxpayers with a greater ability to pay should pay larger amounts. Richer taxpayers should pay more than poorer taxpayers; the hard question is how much more.Vertical equity（垂直公平）：負擔能力越高的人應該繳越多。富人應該比窮人多繳，難的是要多繳多少。
  • Horizontal equity: taxpayers with similar abilities to pay should pay the same amount. The tax law has special provisions (deductions, 扣除額) that change a family's tax based on its circumstances. Problem: it is hard to agree on which factors besides income (e.g. medical bills, tuition) determine ability to pay.Horizontal equity（水平公平）：負擔能力相同的人應該繳一樣多。稅法用特別規定（扣除額）依家庭狀況調整稅額。難處：除了所得，哪些因素（例如醫藥費、學費）會影響負擔能力，很難有共識。

★★ Tax incidence and tax equity租稅歸宿與租稅公平

  • Tax incidence: who bears the burden of taxes. It is central to evaluating tax equity, because the person who bears the burden is not always the person who gets the tax bill. Taxes change supply and demand, so they change equilibrium prices and have indirect effects on employees and consumers.Tax incidence（租稅歸宿）：誰真正承擔稅的負擔。這是評估公平的關鍵，因為真正負擔的人不一定是收到稅單的人。稅改變供需，進而改變均衡價格，間接影響員工和消費者。
  • Example: a tax on fur coats may look vertically equitable (only the rich buy furs). But furs are a luxury with very elastic demand, so the tax shifts demand away from furs and hurts the people who produce furs, who are probably not rich.例子：對皮草大衣課稅看起來符合垂直公平（只有富人買皮草）。但皮草是奢侈品，需求很有彈性，課稅後需求轉走，反而傷到做皮草的人，而他們大概不是富人。
  • Who pays the corporate income tax? People pay all taxes. A corporation is more like a tax collector than a taxpayer: the burden ultimately falls on owners, workers and customers. The tax is popular because it appears to be paid by rich corporations.誰繳公司所得稅？所有的稅最後都是人在繳。公司比較像收稅員而不是納稅人，負擔最後落在股東、員工和顧客身上。這個稅受歡迎，是因為它看起來是有錢的公司在繳。

★★★ The trade-off between equity and efficiency公平與效率的取捨

  • Equity and efficiency are the two most important goals of a tax system, and they often conflict (e.g. the lump-sum tax is the most efficient but the least fair). Political leaders differ in how they weigh this trade-off.公平和效率是稅制最重要的兩個目標，而且常常衝突（例如定額稅最有效率但最不公平）。政治人物對這個取捨的看法各不相同。
  • Economics can help us understand the trade-off and avoid policies that sacrifice efficiency without any increase in equity.經濟學可以幫我們看清這個取捨，並避免犧牲了效率、卻沒有增加公平的政策。

看解答

1. Equity: the poor spend a larger share of income on necessities, so exempting them makes the sales tax less regressive (better vertical equity). Efficiency: demand for necessities is inelastic, so taxing them causes little deadweight loss; exempting them means higher rates on other goods with more elastic demand (more deadweight loss), and it adds administrative burden (deciding what counts as "food" or "clothing"). It is the equity–efficiency trade-off.
2. Not plausible. The rate rises by 20% (5 → 6), so revenue would rise by 20% only if the tax base (sales) did not change. But higher prices reduce the quantity sold, so revenue rises by less than 20%.1. 公平：窮人花在必需品的所得比例較高，排除必需品讓銷售稅比較不累退（垂直公平較好）。效率：必需品需求缺乏彈性，課它的無謂損失小；排除之後其他商品要課更高的稅（無謂損失變大），還多了行政負擔（什麼算「食物」「衣服」）。這就是公平與效率的取捨。2. 不合理：稅率漲 20%（5 → 6），只有在銷售量不變時稅收才會漲 20%。價格上升會減少銷售量，所以稅收增加會少於 20%。

Chapter summarySummary 本章總結
  • The federal government raises revenue mainly with personal income taxes and payroll taxes; state and local governments mainly with sales taxes and property taxes.聯邦政府主要靠個人所得稅和工資稅；州與地方政府主要靠銷售稅和財產稅。
  • The efficiency of a tax system refers to the costs it imposes on taxpayers: the deadweight loss (taxes alter incentives) and the administrative burden of complying.稅制的效率指它加在納稅人身上的成本：無謂損失（稅改變誘因）和遵守稅法的行政負擔。
  • The equity of a tax system concerns whether the burden is distributed fairly: the benefits principle and the ability-to-pay principle (vertical and horizontal equity).稅制的公平看負擔分配得公不公平：受益原則和量能課稅原則（垂直公平、水平公平）。
  • The distribution of tax burdens is not the same as the distribution of tax bills (tax incidence).稅負的分配不等於稅單的分配（租稅歸宿）。
  • Policymakers often face a trade-off between efficiency and equity; much of the debate arises because people weigh the two goals differently.政策制定者常面對效率與公平的取捨；很多爭論來自大家對兩個目標的重視程度不同。
Common trap易錯點
  • Marginal tax rate = the rate on the last dollar; average tax rate = total tax ÷ total income. A question that gives a bracket schedule usually puts the average rate in the options as a trap.邊際稅率是最後一塊錢的稅率；平均稅率 = 總稅額 ÷ 總所得。給級距的題目通常把平均稅率放在選項當陷阱。
  • A lump-sum tax has a marginal rate of 0% and an average rate that falls as income rises: most efficient, but regressive.定額稅的邊際稅率是 0%，平均稅率隨所得下降：最有效率，但是累退。
  • The administrative burden is a kind of deadweight loss, not a tax and not producer surplus.行政負擔是一種無謂損失，不是稅，也不是生產者剩餘。
  • IRA and 401(k) make the U.S. system more like a consumption tax, because saving is not taxed until withdrawn.IRA 和 401(k) 讓美國稅制更像消費稅，因為儲蓄在提領前不課稅。
  • Vertical and horizontal equity both belong to the ability-to-pay principle, not the benefits principle.垂直公平和水平公平都屬於量能課稅原則，不屬於受益原則。
  • Regressive does not mean the rich pay fewer dollars; it means they pay a smaller percentage of income.累退不是富人繳的錢比較少，而是繳的比例比較低。
  • Deadweight loss = lost surplus − tax revenue. Buyers who stop buying because of the tax pay no tax: their lost surplus is the deadweight loss.無謂損失 = 減少的剩餘 − 稅收。因為課稅而不買的人沒繳稅，他們失去的剩餘就是無謂損失。
