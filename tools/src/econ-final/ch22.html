★★★ Hidden actions and moral hazard: the principal-agent problem隱藏行動與道德風險：委託代理問題

  • Moral hazard: the tendency of a person who is imperfectly monitored to engage in dishonest or otherwise undesirable behavior.Moral hazard（道德風險）：沒被完整監督的人，傾向做出不誠實或不好的行為。
  • Examples: workers shirk because the employer cannot continually monitor their effort; someone whose property is insured may not try as hard to protect it from theft or damage; while the parents are out, the babysitter may spend more time watching videos than watching the children.例子：雇主無法隨時監督，員工就偷懶；財產有保險的人比較不努力防竊防損；父母出門時，保母看影片的時間比看小孩還多。
  • Agent: a person performing a task on someone else's behalf (e.g. a worker). Principal: the person for whom the task is performed (e.g. an employer).Agent（代理人）：替別人做事的人（例如員工）。Principal（委託人）：事情為他而做的人（例如雇主）。
  • Principal-agent problem (委託代理問題): when the principal cannot perfectly monitor the agent, there is a risk ("hazard") that the agent does something undesirable ("immoral"), e.g. playing video games or surfing the web while on the clock.Principal-agent problem（委託代理問題）：委託人無法完整監督代理人時，代理人就有做壞事（「不道德」）的「風險」，例如上班時間打電動、上網。

  • Corporate management: the separation of ownership and control creates a principal-agent problem. Principals = the shareholders, who pay managers to maximize profit; agents = the managers, who may pursue their own objectives. Managers are sometimes sent to jail for taking advantage of shareholders (the slides show executives who took kickbacks from suppliers or leaked trade secrets).公司治理：所有權與經營權分離造成委託代理問題。委託人是股東，付錢請經理人追求最大利潤；代理人是經理人，可能追求自己的目標。有經理人因為占股東便宜而坐牢（投影片舉了向供應商收回扣、洩漏營業秘密的高階主管的例子）。
  • Solution: shareholders hire a board of directors (董事會) to oversee management and create incentives for managers to pursue the firm's goals; align managers' interests with the firm's goals, e.g. pay in equity (stock) instead of cash salary.解法：股東請董事會監督經營階層，設計誘因讓經理人追求公司目標；讓經理人的利益和公司一致，例如用股票代替現金薪水。

★★★ Hidden characteristics and adverse selection隱藏特性與逆選擇

Adverse selection arises when the seller knows more than the buyer about the good being sold. The information asymmetry prevents some mutually beneficial trades.Adverse selection（逆選擇）：賣方比買方更了解所賣的東西。資訊不對稱讓一些對雙方都有利的交易做不成。

Example 1: the market for used carsThe seller knows more about the car's quality. Owners of "lemons" (bad cars) are more likely to sell, so buyers avoid used cars, and owners of good used cars cannot get a fair price, so they may not bother selling.例 1：二手車市場。賣方比較了解車況。爛車車主比較想賣，買方就不敢買二手車；好車車主賣不到合理價格，乾脆不賣。
Example 2: insuranceBuyers of health insurance know more about their health. People with hidden health problems have more incentive to buy, so prices reflect a sicker-than-average person, and these prices discourage healthy people from buying.例 2：保險。買健康保險的人比保險公司更了解自己的健康。有隱藏疾病的人比較想買，保費就反映比平均更病的人的成本，這個價格又把健康的人趕走。

★★★ Political economy: the Condorcet voting paradox政治經濟學：康多塞投票悖論

  • Political economy: applies the methods of economics to study how government works.Political economy（政治經濟學）：用經濟學方法研究政府如何運作。
  • Condorcet paradox (孔多塞投票悖論): the failure of majority rule to produce transitive preferences for society. Transitivity: if A is preferred to B and B is preferred to C, then A should be preferred to C.Condorcet paradox（康多塞悖論）：多數決無法產生有遞移性的社會偏好。Transitivity（遞移性）：A 優於 B、B 優於 C，則 A 應該優於 C。

★★★ Arrow's impossibility theoremArrow 不可能定理

Arrow's four desirable properties of a voting system投票制度的四個理想性質Meaning意義
1. Unanimity一致同意If everyone prefers A to B, then A should beat B.每個人都偏好 A 勝過 B，A 就應該贏 B。
2. Transitivity遞移性If A beats B and B beats C, then A should beat C.A 贏 B、B 贏 C，A 就應該贏 C。
3. Independence of irrelevant alternatives不相關選項獨立性The ranking between any two outcomes should not depend on whether a third option is available.任兩個選項的排名，不應該因為第三個選項在不在而改變。
4. No dictators沒有獨裁者No person always gets his way regardless of everyone else's preferences.沒有人不管別人偏好怎樣都永遠如願。

Arrow's impossibility theorem: a mathematical result showing that, under certain assumed conditions, no voting system can satisfy all four properties: there is no scheme for aggregating individual preferences into a valid set of social preferences.Arrow's impossibility theorem（Arrow 不可能定理）：數學結果，在某些假設下，沒有任何投票制度能同時滿足四個性質：無法把個人偏好加總成一套合理的社會偏好。

★★★ The median voter theorem中位選民定理

  • Suppose society is deciding the size of the government budget. Each voter has a preferred budget. Line up all voters in order of their preferences: the median voter is the one right in the middle.假設社會要決定政府預算的大小，每個選民有自己理想的預算。把所有選民照偏好排好，正中間那位就是中位選民。
  • Median voter theorem: majority rule will always pick the outcome most preferred by the median voter. In a two-way race, the option closest to the median voter's favorite wins.Median voter theorem（中位選民定理）：多數決一定會選出中位選民最喜歡的結果。二選一時，最接近中位選民偏好的選項會贏。
{{FIG_MEDIAN}}
  • Implications: in a two-party or two-candidate race, each party moves its position toward the median voter; minority views are not given much weight; the theorem may fail (e.g. when preferences are not lined up on one dimension).意涵：兩黨或兩位候選人競爭時，雙方都會往中位選民靠攏；少數意見不太受重視；這個定理也可能失靈（例如偏好不能排在同一條線上時）。
  • Note: the median is not the mode (the most popular choice) and not the average. In homework #9, $20 has the most voters, but the median voter (the 5th of 9) wants $18, and $18 beats every other amount.注意：中位數不是眾數（最多人選的），也不是平均數。作業第 9 題 $20 的人最多，但中位選民（9 人中第 5 位）要 $18，$18 能贏過任何其他金額。

c. Sorted 0, 1, 2, 3, 4: the median roommate (Ridley) wants 2.
d. 3 vs 2: Quentin and Spike vote for 3; Ridley, Martin and Steven vote for 2. 2 wins, 3 to 2: majority rule picks the median voter's choice, not the efficient one.a. 有效率 = 3 部（前三部對整群人的價值都超過 $15）。b. 價值 ≥ $3 就想看：Quentin 4、Spike 3、Ridley 2、Martin 1、Steven 0。c. 排好 0、1、2、3、4，中位室友（Ridley）要 2 部。d. 3 對 2：Quentin、Spike 投 3；Ridley、Martin、Steven 投 2。2 部以 3 比 2 勝出：多數決選出的是中位選民的偏好，不是有效率的結果。

★ Politicians are people too政治人物也是人

  • Politicians are motivated by self-interest, just like firms and consumers: some are motivated by re-election and willing to sacrifice the national interest for it; others by greed.政治人物和廠商、消費者一樣受自利驅動：有些為了連任願意犧牲國家利益，有些是因為貪婪。
  • Lesson: economic policy is not made by benevolent leaders, and sometimes fails to resemble the ideals derived in economics textbooks.教訓：經濟政策不是由仁慈的領袖制定的，有時和教科書推導的理想差很遠。

★★★ Behavioral economics行為經濟學

Behavioral economics: a subfield of economics that integrates the insights of psychology. People aren't always as rational as traditional models assume.Behavioral economics（行為經濟學）：結合心理學觀點的經濟學分支。人不一定像傳統模型假設的那麼理性。

  • Herbert Simon viewed humans as satisficers: people who make choices that are merely "good enough" rather than optimal. Others say people are only "near rational" (近似理性) or show "bounded rationality" (有限理性).Herbert Simon 認為人是satisficers（滿意者）：只做「夠好」而不是最佳的選擇。也有人說人只是「近似理性」或「有限理性」。

看解答

Predicted (rational players): A proposes 99–1 and B accepts, because $1 is better than nothing.
Actual experiments: B usually rejects lopsided splits like 99–1 as wildly unfair. Expecting this, A usually offers $30 or $40; B sees it as unfair, but not enough to give up his self-interest, so B accepts.理性預測：A 提 99 比 1，B 接受，因為 $1 總比 0 好。實際實驗：B 通常會拒絕 99 比 1 這種太不公平的分法。A 預料到，通常給 $30 或 $40；B 雖覺得不公平，但還不至於放棄自己的利益，所以接受。

Chapter summarySummary 本章總結
  • In many transactions, information is asymmetric. With hidden actions, principals worry that agents suffer from moral hazard; with hidden characteristics, buyers worry about adverse selection among sellers.很多交易中資訊不對稱。有隱藏行動時，委託人擔心代理人有道德風險；有隱藏特性時，買方擔心賣方的逆選擇。
  • Private markets sometimes deal with asymmetric information through signaling and screening.民間市場有時用訊號和篩選處理資訊不對稱。
  • Governments are imperfect institutions: the Condorcet paradox shows majority rule may not produce transitive social preferences; Arrow's impossibility theorem shows no voting system is perfect.政府是不完美的機構：康多塞悖論顯示多數決可能沒有遞移性；Arrow 不可能定理顯示沒有完美的投票制度。
  • In many situations majority voting produces the outcome the median voter wants, regardless of everyone else; policymakers may be motivated by self-interest.很多情況下多數決會產生中位選民想要的結果，不管其他人怎麼想；政策制定者可能出於自利。
  • People are not always rational, care about fairness (even to their own detriment), and can be inconsistent over time.人不一定理性、在乎公平（甚至犧牲自己）、而且前後可能不一致。
Common trap易錯點
  • Hidden action → moral hazard (after the deal: the agent shirks, the insured gets careless). Hidden characteristic → adverse selection (before the deal: what is on sale is worse).隱藏行動 → 道德風險（交易之後：代理人偷懶、保了險就不小心）。隱藏特性 → 逆選擇（交易之前：拿出來賣的比較差）。
  • Signaling is done by the informed side (seller's warranty, degree, advertising); screening by the uninformed side (physical exam, mechanic's inspection, deductible menu, phone plans).訊號是有資訊的一方做的（賣家保固、文憑、廣告）；篩選是沒資訊的一方做的（體檢、技師驗車、自負額方案、電信方案）。
  • A safer car or insurance can make people take more risks: that is moral hazard, not adverse selection (the Volvo question).更安全的車或有保險會讓人更敢冒險：這是道德風險，不是逆選擇（Volvo 那題）。
  • "The order of voting matters" is the lesson of the Condorcet paradox; "no voting system is perfect" is Arrow's theorem.「表決順序會影響結果」是康多塞悖論的教訓；「沒有完美的投票制度」是 Arrow 定理。
  • For a pairwise agenda, work round by round: in each round add up the % of voters who rank one option above the other.兩兩表決要一輪一輪算：每一輪把「把這個排在那個前面」的選民比例加起來。
  • The median voter is the one in the middle of the ordered list, not the most common choice and not the average.中位選民是排好之後正中間的那位，不是最多人選的，也不是平均數。
  • Remedies for moral hazard: monitoring, higher (efficiency) wages, delayed payment. Stopping bonuses is not one.道德風險的對策：監督、高（效率）工資、延後付款。停發獎金不是。
