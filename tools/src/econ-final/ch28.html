The u-rate = unemployed ÷ labor force. Even in good times there is always some unemployment: frictional (it takes time to find the right job) + structural (the wage is above equilibrium, so there are not enough jobs) = the natural rate. The gap between the actual rate and the natural rate is cyclical unemployment. Wages stay above equilibrium because of minimum wages, unions and efficiency wages.失業率 = 失業人數 ÷ 勞動力。就算景氣好也一定有失業：摩擦性（找到合適工作要時間）＋結構性（工資高於均衡，工作不夠分）= 自然失業率。實際失業率和自然失業率的差距是週期性失業。工資會停在均衡之上，原因是最低工資、工會、效率工資。

★★★ Labor force statistics: who counts as unemployed勞動力統計：誰算失業

  • Produced by the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) in the U.S. Department of Labor, from a regular survey of 60,000 households, based on the adult population (16 years or older).由美國勞工部的Bureau of Labor Statistics（勞工統計局，BLS）發布，資料來自定期調查 6 萬戶家庭，以成年人口（16 歲以上）為基礎。
  • The BLS divides the adult population into 3 groups:BLS 把成年人口分成 3 組：
    • Employed: paid employees, the self-employed, and unpaid workers in a family business.Employed（就業者）：受薪員工、自營者，以及在家族事業無薪幫忙的人。
    • Unemployed: people not working who have looked for work in the past 4 weeks.Unemployed（失業者）：沒有工作、而且過去 4 週內有找工作的人。
    • Not in the labor force: everyone else (students, homemakers, retirees, discouraged workers).Not in the labor force（非勞動力）：其他所有人（學生、家管、退休者、怯志工作者）。
  • Labor force = Employed + Unemployed: the total number of workers.Labor force（勞動力）= 就業者 + 失業者：工作者的總數。

★★ The duration of unemployment失業持續期間

  • Most spells of unemployment are short (BLS employment situation survey): typically 1/3 of the unemployed have been unemployed under 5 weeks, 2/3 under 14 weeks; only 20% over 6 months.大部分失業期間都很短（BLS 就業狀況調查）：通常 1/3 的失業者失業不到 5 週，2/3 不到 14 週；只有 20% 超過 6 個月。
  • Yet most observed unemployment is long term: the small group of long-term unemployed has little turnover, so it accounts for most of the unemployment observed over time.但任何時刻觀察到的失業大多是長期失業：少數長期失業者幾乎不流動，所以佔了長期以來觀察到的大部分失業。
  • Knowing these facts helps policymakers design better policies to help the unemployed.知道這些事實，有助於政策制定者設計更好的失業協助政策。
Example (p24): 55 unemployed people in a yearYou meet 55 unemployed people over a year. 52 of them (52/55 = 95%) are unemployed for one week; 3 are unemployed for the whole year. Whenever you walk into the unemployment office you meet 4 people: the one-week person of that week plus the 3 long-term people, so 3 of the 4 (75%) are unemployed for the entire year.例子（p24）：一年遇到 55 個失業者 52 人（95%）只失業一週，3 人失業一整年。每次走進就業服務處，會看到 4 個人：當週那位一週失業者＋3 位長期失業者，所以 4 人中有 3 人（75%）失業一整年。

Even though 95% of spells end in one week, 75% of the unemployment observed at any moment belongs to those unemployed for a full year.雖然 95% 的失業期一週就結束，任何時刻看到的失業有 75% 是失業一整年的人。「人次」大多短期，「某一時點的存量」大多長期。

Even when the economy is doing well, there is always some unemployment.就算經濟很好，也一定有一些失業。

★★★ Job search and sectoral shifts (the main cause of frictional unemployment)找工作與部門移轉（摩擦性失業的主要原因）

  • Workers have different tastes and skills, and jobs have different requirements. Job search: the process of matching workers with appropriate jobs.工作者的偏好和技能不同，工作的要求也不同。Job search（找工作）：把工作者和合適的工作配對的過程。
  • Sectoral shifts: changes in the composition of demand across industries or regions. They displace some workers, who must search for new jobs suited to their skills and tastes.Sectoral shifts（部門移轉）：各產業或地區之間需求組成的改變，會讓部分工人失業，他們必須重新找適合的工作。
  • Examples: technological change raises demand for computer repair persons and lowers demand for typewriter repair persons; a new trade agreement raises demand for workers in export sectors and lowers it in import-competing sectors.例子：技術改變讓電腦維修人員需求增加、打字機維修人員需求減少；新的貿易協定讓出口部門的工人需求增加、和進口競爭的部門需求減少。
  • It takes time for workers to change sectors, so sectoral shifts cause frictional unemployment. In a dynamic economy smaller shifts happen frequently.工人轉換部門需要時間，所以部門移轉造成摩擦性失業。動態經濟中小規模的移轉經常發生。

★★ Unemployment insurance失業保險

  • Unemployment insurance (UI): a government program that partially protects workers' incomes when they become unemployed.Unemployment insurance（失業保險，UI）：失業時部分保障工人所得的政府計畫。
  • UI increases frictional unemployment: people respond to incentives. Benefits end when a worker takes a job, so workers have less incentive to search or to take jobs while eligible for benefits.UI 會增加摩擦性失業：人會對誘因做出反應。一找到工作給付就停止，所以領給付期間工人比較沒有誘因去找工作或接受工作。
  • Benefits of UI: it reduces uncertainty over incomes, and gives the unemployed more time to search, resulting in better job matches and thus higher productivity (「人盡其才，地盡其利，物盡其用，貨暢其流」).UI 的好處：降低所得的不確定性，讓失業者有更多時間找，配對更好，生產力因此更高（人盡其才）。

★★★ Structural unemployment: the wage is kept above equilibrium (1. minimum-wage laws)結構性失業：工資被維持在均衡之上（1. 最低工資法）

Structural unemployment occurs when there are not enough jobs to go around, because the wage is kept above equilibrium: at the actual wage W₁ the quantity of labor supplied exceeds the quantity demanded. There are three reasons for this: minimum-wage laws, unions and efficiency wages.結構性失業發生在工作不夠分的時候，原因是工資被維持在均衡之上：在實際工資 W₁，勞動供給量大於勞動需求量。原因有三個：最低工資、工會、效率工資。

{{FIG_STRUCT}}
  • Minimum wage: may exceed the equilibrium wage for the least skilled or experienced workers, causing structural unemployment.Minimum wage（最低工資）：可能高於技能或經驗最少的工人的均衡工資，造成結構性失業。
  • But this group is a small part of the labor force, so the minimum wage can't explain most unemployment.但這群人只佔勞動力的一小部分，所以最低工資無法解釋大部分的失業。

★★★ 2. Unions2. 工會

  • Union: a worker association that bargains with employers over wages, benefits and working conditions, using its market power to negotiate higher wages. The typical union worker earns about 10%–20% higher wages and more benefits than a nonunion worker doing the same type of work.Union（工會）：和雇主就工資、福利、工作條件議價的工人組織，用市場力量爭取較高工資。一般工會工人的工資比做同樣工作的非工會工人高約 10%–20%，福利也較多。
  • Unions raise the wage above equilibrium → the quantity of labor demanded falls → unemployment results.工會把工資提高到均衡之上 → 勞動需求量下降 → 產生失業。
  • Insiders: workers who remain employed, better off. Outsiders: workers who lose their jobs, worse off. Some outsiders go to non-unionized labor markets, which increases labor supply and reduces wages there.Insiders（局內人）：保住工作的人，變好。Outsiders（局外人）：丟了工作的人，變差。有些局外人轉去沒有工會的勞動市場，讓那裡的勞動供給增加、工資下降。

Are unions good or bad? Economists disagree.工會是好是壞？經濟學家意見不一。
Critics批評者Unions are cartels. They raise wages above equilibrium, which causes unemployment and/or depresses wages in non-union labor markets.工會是卡特爾。把工資拉到均衡之上，造成失業，或壓低非工會市場的工資。
Advocates支持者Unions counter the market power of large firms and make firms more responsive to workers' concerns.工會能對抗大企業的市場力量，讓企業更重視工人的需求。

★★★ 3. Efficiency wages3. 效率工資

The theory of efficiency wages: firms voluntarily pay above-equilibrium wages to boost worker productivity. Different versions give different reasons:Efficiency wages（效率工資）理論：廠商自願付高於均衡的工資來提高工人生產力。不同版本有不同理由：

Reason理由How a higher wage helps the firm高工資怎麼幫到廠商
1. Worker health工人健康In less developed countries poor nutrition is common; higher wages let workers eat better, so they are healthier and more productive.在開發中國家營養不良很常見；高工資讓工人吃得好、更健康、生產力更高。
2. Worker turnover工人流動Hiring and training new workers is costly; high wages give workers more incentive to stay, so turnover falls.雇用與訓練新人很花錢；高工資讓工人更想留下，降低離職率。
3. Worker quality工人品質Higher wages attract better job applicants, raising the quality of the workforce.高工資吸引較好的應徵者，提高員工素質。
4. Worker effort工人努力Workers can work hard or shirk; shirkers are fired if caught. Being fired is a good deterrent only if another job is hard to find; with the wage above equilibrium there aren't enough jobs to go around, so workers have more incentive to work, not shirk.工人可以努力也可以偷懶，被抓到就開除。開除有沒有嚇阻力，要看找新工作難不難；工資高於均衡時工作不夠分，工人就比較不敢偷懶。

★★ The natural rate: a summary, and are we back at full employment?自然失業率總整理，以及回到充分就業了嗎？

FrictionalIt takes time to search for the right jobs, even if there are enough jobs to go around. Raised by sectoral shifts and UI; lowered by employment agencies, training and better matching.找到對的工作要時間，就算工作夠分也會有。部門移轉、失業保險會提高它；就業服務、職訓、更好的配對會降低它。
StructuralThe wage is above equilibrium, so there are not enough jobs. Three reasons: minimum wages, labor unions, efficiency wages.工資高於均衡，工作不夠分。三個原因：最低工資、工會、效率工資。
  • Cyclical unemployment, the short-term fluctuations associated with business cycles, comes in later chapters.週期性失業（和景氣循環有關的短期波動）在之後的章節。
  • Eye on Full Employment: the Atlanta Fed suggests the Z-Pop ratio, the percentage of the working-age population that is fully utilized: people working full time, working part time for a noneconomic reason, and people who say they don't want a job.Eye on Full Employment：亞特蘭大聯邦準備銀行建議用 Z-Pop ratio：工作年齡人口中被充分利用的比例，包括全職工作者、因非經濟原因做兼職的人、以及說自己不想要工作的人。
  • Before the 2008–2009 recession 95.2% was fully utilized; it fell, then slowly climbed to 94.5%. The Z-Pop ratio gives almost the same answer as comparing the u-rate with the natural rate, but it shows a small amount of underused labor remaining in 2016.2008–2009 衰退前是 95.2%，之後下降，再慢慢回升到 94.5%。Z-Pop 和「比較失業率與自然失業率」的答案幾乎一樣，但它顯示 2016 年仍有少量未被充分利用的勞動力。

Final mock question (p66): Moscow's anti-smoking campaignMuscovites smoke less and chew gum more. Tobacco companies lay off workers, while chewing-gum manufacturers employ more workers. This is an example of a. frictional unemployment created by efficiency wages. b. frictional unemployment created by sectoral shifts. c. structural unemployment created by efficiency wages. d. structural unemployment created by sectoral shifts.期末考模擬試題（p66）：莫斯科戒菸運動 莫斯科人少抽菸、多嚼口香糖；菸草公司裁員、口香糖廠多雇人。這是 a. 效率工資造成的摩擦性失業 b. 部門移轉造成的摩擦性失業 c. 效率工資造成的結構性失業 d. 部門移轉造成的結構性失業
看解答

Answer: b. Demand moved from one industry to another (a sectoral shift), and laid-off workers need time to find new jobs: that is frictional unemployment. Sectoral shifts are a cause of frictional, not structural, unemployment.答案 b。需求從一個產業移到另一個產業（部門移轉），被裁的工人要花時間找新工作，這是摩擦性失業。部門移轉造成的是摩擦性失業，不是結構性失業。

Appendix Question 10 (p67–71): an employer mandate of $4 per hourCongress requires employers to provide a benefit (such as healthcare) that raises the cost of an employee by $4 per hour. (a) Effect on the demand for labor? (b) If employees value the benefit exactly at its cost, effect on the supply of labor? (c) If the wage adjusts freely, how does the law affect the wage and employment? Are employers and employees better or worse off? (d) If, before the mandate, the wage was $3 above the minimum wage, how does the mandate affect the wage, employment and unemployment? (e) If workers do not value the benefit at all, how do the answers to (b) and (c) change?Appendix Question 10（p67–71）：每小時 $4 的雇主強制福利 國會要求雇主提供某項福利（例如健保），讓每位員工每小時成本多 $4。(a) 對勞動需求？(b) 員工對福利的評價剛好等於成本時，對勞動供給？(c) 工資可自由調整時，工資與就業？雇主、員工變好或變差？(d) 原本工資比最低工資高 $3，對工資、就業、失業？(e) 員工完全不重視這項福利時，(b)(c) 怎麼改？
看解答

(a) Each worker now costs $4 more, so the demand curve shifts down by $4 (firms will pay a wage $4 lower for each quantity).
(b) Workers value the benefit at $4, so they will work the same hours for a wage $4 lower: the supply curve shifts down by $4.
(c) Both curves shift down by $4 → the wage falls by exactly $4 and employment is unchanged. Employers pay $4 less in wages plus $4 of benefit, the same cost; workers get $4 less in wages plus a benefit worth $4. Both are no better and no worse off.
(d) The wage can fall only $3 before it hits the minimum wage. The wage stays at the minimum wage, $1 above the new equilibrium → employment falls and unemployment rises (labor surplus).
(e) (b) The supply curve does not shift. (c) Only demand shifts down: the wage falls by less than $4, employment falls, and both employers and employees are worse off (a deadweight loss, like a tax).(a) 每位員工多花 $4，需求線往下移 $4。(b) 員工覺得福利值 $4，願意少拿 $4 工資做一樣多，供給線往下移 $4。(c) 兩條線都下移 $4 → 工資剛好降 $4、就業不變；雇主總成本一樣，員工工資少 $4 但多了值 $4 的福利，雙方不變好也不變差。(d) 工資只能降 $3 就碰到最低工資，停在比新均衡高 $1 的地方 → 就業下降、失業上升。(e) (b) 供給線不動；(c) 只有需求下移，工資降不到 $4、就業下降，雇主和員工都變差（像課稅一樣有無謂損失）。

Chapter summarySummary 本章總結
  • The unemployment rate is the percentage of those who would like to work who do not have jobs.失業率是想工作的人當中沒有工作的比例。
  • Unemployment and labor-force participation vary widely across demographic groups.失業率和勞參率在不同人口族群之間差異很大。
  • The natural rate of unemployment is the normal rate around which the actual rate fluctuates. Cyclical unemployment is the deviation from the natural rate and is connected to short-term economic fluctuations.自然失業率是實際失業率上下波動的正常水準。週期性失業是偏離自然失業率的部分，和短期經濟波動有關。
  • The natural rate includes frictional and structural unemployment. Frictional unemployment occurs when workers take time to search for the right jobs.自然失業率包含摩擦性和結構性失業。摩擦性失業是工人花時間找對的工作而產生。
  • Structural unemployment occurs when above-equilibrium wages result in a surplus of labor; three reasons: minimum-wage laws, unions and efficiency wages.結構性失業是工資高於均衡造成勞動過剩；三個原因：最低工資、工會、效率工資。
Common trap易錯點
  • The u-rate divides by the labor force; the participation rate divides by the adult population. Don't swap them.失業率除以勞動力，勞參率除以成年人口，不要搞反。
  • A discouraged worker is not in the labor force, so when someone gives up looking the u-rate falls (and the participation rate falls too).怯志工作者是非勞動力，所以有人放棄找工作時失業率下降（勞參率也下降）。
  • A part-time worker counts as employed, even if he wants a full-time job.兼職工作者算就業，就算他想要全職。
  • Sectoral shifts and unemployment insurance → frictional; minimum wage, unions, efficiency wages → structural.部門移轉、失業保險 → 摩擦性；最低工資、工會、效率工資 → 結構性。
  • Natural rate = frictional + structural (not cyclical). In a recession cyclical unemployment is positive; in a boom it is negative.自然失業率 = 摩擦性 + 結構性（不含週期性）。衰退時週期性失業為正，繁榮時為負。
  • Efficiency wages are paid voluntarily by firms; minimum wages and union wages are not the firm's choice.效率工資是廠商自願付的；最低工資和工會工資不是廠商自己選的。
  • Most spells of unemployment are short, but most unemployment observed at a moment is long term.大部分失業期很短，但某一時點看到的失業大多是長期的。
